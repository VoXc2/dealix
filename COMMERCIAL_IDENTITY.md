## Qualification truth

A company becomes commercially qualified only when evidence supports the current canonical criteria, including:

- specific painful problem;
- accountable decision owner;
- lawful usable data or explicit missing-evidence path;
- measurable baseline/proof path;
- approval willingness;
- budget/timing discussion;
- no prohibited guarantee, data, compliance-bypass or uncontrolled-automation request.

`Research != relationship`.  
`Public contact != consent`.  
`Meeting != qualified problem`.  
`Draft != sent`.

## Evidence gates

### Revenue
Requires approved commercial scope, an authorized invoice/payment request actually issued where applicable, and verified payment-received evidence reconciled to the same customer/obligation.

### Delivery
Requires a valid scope/obligation, tracked completion, delivery evidence and customer acceptance where applicable.

### Customer value
Requires a source-bound baseline, measured outcome/window, same-customer evidence, attribution caveats/confidence, and customer acceptance when the claim implies customer-confirmed value.

Synthetic/demo/internal proof never becomes customer proof merely because it is persuasive.
