## Visual grammar

Use:

- forward vectors;
- controlled rails / channels;
- evidence nodes;
- signal convergence;
- structured grids;
- generous negative space;
- restrained data geometry;
- proof-class labels;
- high-contrast light and dark executive surfaces.

Avoid:

- robot heads, glowing brains, circuit clichés and sparkle AI motifs;
- neon overload;
- decorative national marks inside the logo;
- fake dashboards or invented customer metrics;
- a unique visual identity for every product, agent, event or campaign.

## Masterbrand architecture

**Dealix** is the one masterbrand.

Capability families such as Command OS, Revenue OS, Proof OS, Governance OS and Data OS are navigation/product families under Dealix, not separate companies or visual brands.

The market-facing company story is organized around executable problems and recurring economic motions, not around a fixed list of eight visual sub-brands.

## Commercial front door

Current public path:

**Free Mini Diagnostic → Qualified Discovery → Customer-Specific Quote → 30-Day Revenue Command Pilot → Proof → Stop / Expand / Redesign**

No historical 499/999/1500 SAR offer, public checkout, guarantee, refund promise or old sprint duration becomes current authority merely because a legacy document still exists.

## Buying-group adaptation

Every material B2B asset should remain the same masterbrand while adapting the evidence to the decision maker:

- Founder / GM: priority, control, speed, decision clarity
- Revenue leader: leakage, follow-up, pipeline execution, proof
- Operations: ownership, workflow burden, acceptance criteria
- Finance / procurement: bounded economics, reversibility, commercial clarity
- IT / security: integration boundary, auditability, least privilege
- Legal / governance: consent, provenance, suppression, approvals, evidence
