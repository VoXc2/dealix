## 2. The mark — D + Forward Signal

The approved symbol is a geometric **D** containing a forward cyan signal.

- **D** = Dealix, deals, decisions and durable business value.
- **Forward Signal** = `Signal -> Decision -> Action`.
- **Negative space** = governance, approval boundaries and controlled execution.
- **Open forward direction** = turning opportunity into measurable outcome.

The mark intentionally avoids generic robot, brain, circuit and sparkle symbolism. It should read as a business/technology identity before it reads as an "AI logo".

The wordmark should remain clean and restrained. Avoid decorative AI display fonts.

## 7. Usage rules

The mark must:

1. remain recognizable at 16px / 24px / 32px;
2. work in one color;
3. work on light and dark backgrounds;
4. remain identifiable without gradients;
5. remain recognizable without the wordmark;
6. never be stretched, skewed or placed inside another arbitrary badge;
7. never use an unapproved alternate palette as a new authority.

Minimum clear space around the mark: approximately one quarter of the mark width.  
Recommended minimum horizontal-lockup width: 160px digital.
