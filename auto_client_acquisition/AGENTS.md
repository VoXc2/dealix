This scoped file applies to `auto_client_acquisition/**` and refines the root `AGENTS.md`.

## Truth boundary
Company Brain, acquisition research and Opportunity Graph workflows must preserve current commercial truth and provenance.

- Default retrieval authority is CURRENT_ONLY; historical material remains provenance and must not silently become current pricing, architecture, offer, proof or relationship authority.
- Free diagnostic -> qualified discovery -> customer-specific quote. Historical fixed prices/durations are reference/history only.
- Research != relationship; public contact != consent; signal != qualified problem; draft != sent; quote != invoice; invoice != payment; payment != verified revenue; delivery != customer value; customer value != public proof.
- Aggregate Saudi market statistics prioritize research only. Never promote them into named buyer intent/pain/pipeline.
- No cold WhatsApp, no personal LinkedIn scraping/automation, no deceptive outreach.
- Customer-provided facts remain customer context even when they mention old prices/agent counts; do not rewrite user/customer evidence to fit Dealix policy.
- Current Dealix architecture is Agentic Holding / Sector Companies / Arm Pods / logical agents, not a fixed-five product.
- Automatic model execution is NO_DEEPSEEK and only through canonical model/cost/data authority; acquisition workers cannot choose paid/provider routes themselves.

External sends, quote commitments, payments, publication and other material effects remain exact action-bound L5. Draft/internal preparation may proceed autonomously.
