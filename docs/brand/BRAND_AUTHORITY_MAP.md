# Dealix Brand Authority Map

Status: PROPOSED CANONICAL BRAND EXPRESSION CONTRACT  
Date: 2026-08-28

## Purpose

Prevent brand, commercial, visual, claims, channel and proof drift across Dealix. This file does not replace higher-priority company or commercial truth. It tells every human, agent, renderer and channel **which source is allowed to decide which question**.

## Authority order by decision type

### 1. Company/category identity
Authority: `docs/00_platform_truth/PLATFORM_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`

Canonical company identity:

> Dealix is a Saudi-first AI Business Operating System.

Commercial wedge:

> Revenue + Proof + Command.

Rule: sell the wedge; build the platform; never reduce the company identity to a revenue tool, chatbot, CRM, agency or generic AI-automation vendor.

### 2. Commercial offer, pricing and launch authority
Authority, in order:
1. `docs/DEALIX_BUSINESS_MODEL.md`
2. `COMMERCIAL_IDENTITY.md`
3. `dealix/config/first_launch_offer_gate.yaml`
4. lower-priority executable metadata only within their explicit scope

The most restrictive current authority wins.

Current public path:

`Free Mini Diagnostic -> Qualified Discovery -> Customer-Specific Quote -> 30-Day Revenue Command Pilot -> Proof -> Stop/Expand/Redesign`

No historical 499/999/1500 SAR offer, 7-day sprint/pilot, public tier table, checkout, guarantee or money-back language may become current authority merely because it exists in the repository or search index.

### 3. Brand expression
Authority: `docs/brand/DEALIX_BRAND_OS.md` plus its directly referenced brand files.

This layer governs message architecture, voice, visual grammar, buying-group adaptation and public asset QA. It cannot weaken platform or commercial truth.

### 4. Claims and proof
Authority:
- `docs/brand/CLAIMS_GUARDRAILS.md`
- canonical proof/evidence systems and current proof records

No public asset may promote synthetic/demo evidence into customer proof, activity into outcome, invoice into payment, or estimate into verified value.

### 5. Active visual implementation
Until an explicitly approved migration replaces it, the current canonical `landing/index.html` visual language is the active public implementation reference.

Verified current tokens in the canonical landing source:
- Ink Navy `#0F172A`
- Deep Teal `#164E63`
- Signal Cyan `#22D3EE`
- Cloud / near-white `#F8FAFC`
- Slate family for neutral text/lines
- Arabic: `IBM Plex Sans Arabic`
- Latin: `Inter`

Historical Navy/Gold, Emerald/Gold and Saudi-Green/Gold documents remain reference/history unless and until deliberately migrated into the canonical public implementation and this authority map is updated.

Semantic success/warning/error colors are status colors, not alternate brand palettes.

### 6. Renderers and channel adapters
Canva, presentation templates, social templates, video renderers, CMS, n8n and platform APIs are renderers/adapters, never brand authority.

Every reusable asset should preserve:
- `content_id`
- `brand_version`
- `claim_version`
- `template_version`
- audience / buying-group role
- evidence/source references
- CTA
- channel
- lifecycle state
- UTM identity when clickable

## Masterbrand architecture

Use **Dealix** as the masterbrand.

Command OS, Revenue OS, Proof OS, Governance OS, Data OS and later systems are product/capability families under Dealix, not independent visual brands.

`Revenue + Proof + Command` is the first market wedge, not a second company name.

Do not create a distinct logo/color system for every OS, channel, event, agent or campaign.

## Buying-group brand requirement

A B2B asset is incomplete if it only persuades one champion. For material opportunities, adapt the same evidence-backed Dealix story to the concerns of:
- Founder / GM: priority, control, speed, decision clarity
- Revenue leader: leakage, follow-up, pipeline execution, proof
- Operations: ownership, workflow burden, acceptance criteria
- Finance / procurement: bounded economics, commercial clarity, reversibility
- IT / security: integration boundary, control, auditability, least privilege
- Legal / governance: consent, provenance, approvals, suppression, evidence

The brand must make the decision easy to defend internally, not merely exciting externally.

## Proof ladder

Public proof must state its class:
1. `INTERNAL_CAPABILITY_PROOF`
2. `SYNTHETIC_OR_DEMO_PROOF`
3. `RUNTIME_OR_PRODUCTION_PROOF`
4. `CUSTOMER_DELIVERY_PROOF`
5. `CUSTOMER_OUTCOME_PROOF`

Classes 4-5 require real same-customer evidence and the permissions appropriate to publication.

## Saudi-first expression

Saudi-first means:
- Arabic is a first-class authored experience, not a literal afterthought.
- use Saudi business language and operating realities when actually relevant.
- avoid decorative national clichés, unsupported government-access claims and token localization.
- compliance language must be scoped and evidenced.

Prefer `PDPL-aware`, `PDPL-governed controls`, `designed around consent/provenance/approval controls`, or an exact verified statement over blanket `PDPL-compliant` claims.

## Legacy / reference policy

The repository contains multiple historical brand systems. They are not deleted merely for being historical, but they cannot silently become public authority.

A legacy brand document may be used for inspiration only when it does not conflict with this map or higher authority. Any migration of a legacy element must be deliberate, reviewed and versioned.

## Distribution principle

One evidence-backed thesis may generate many native channel assets, but the channels must not fragment the brand.

- Website: category, trust, proof and conversion
- Founder LinkedIn: operator POV and human trust; manual-native interaction
- Dealix company channels: product, method, architecture and proof
- YouTube/long video: explanations, walkthroughs and evidence
- Short video: one problem / one operating insight / one CTA
- Search/AEO: durable buyer-problem knowledge
- Events: real relationship acquisition
- Email/WhatsApp: only when channel/relationship/consent authority permits

## Trademark / legal identity

Brand strategy does not equal legal clearance. Before material paid scale, formal incorporation claims, trademark enforcement or costly rebranding commitments, run exact legal/trademark clearance through the appropriate Saudi and international processes. Until then do not claim that `Dealix` is registered or globally cleared unless evidence exists.

## Non-negotiables

- no fake proof, logos, testimonials, customers or outcomes
- no guaranteed revenue/ROI
- no generic `AI automation agency` positioning
- no blanket compliance certification claim without exact evidence
- no renderer/platform inventing its own palette or commercial offer
- no historical public pricing returning through SEO, decks, agents or templates
- no channel-specific visual identity fragmentation
