# Dealix Positioning

## Canonical category

> **Dealix is a Saudi-first AI Business Operating System.**

This is inherited from `docs/00_platform_truth/PLATFORM_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md` and must not be weakened or replaced by a narrower brand document.

## First commercial wedge

> **Revenue + Proof + Command.**

The wedge is how Dealix enters the market. It is not the full company identity.

## Market problem

Saudi B2B companies often do not need another disconnected AI demo. They need a governed operating layer that can understand company context, identify what matters, prepare or control execution, preserve accountability, and show what evidence actually supports the result.

## Positioning statement

For Saudi B2B companies that need to improve execution without handing uncontrolled authority to AI, Dealix provides a governed AI Business Operating System that connects company context, opportunities, decisions, approvals, actions, outcomes and proof in one operating loop.

Dealix starts with a bounded **Revenue + Proof + Command** workflow, then expands only after evidence supports the next scope.

## Category contrast

Dealix is not positioned as:
- a generic chatbot;
- a CRM replacement;
- a lead scraper;
- an AI automation agency;
- an uncontrolled autonomous-agent platform;
- a guaranteed-revenue service.

The contrast should not be used to make unsupported superiority claims. Explain the operating mechanism instead.

## Differentiation stack

Dealix is differentiated by the combination of:
1. **Saudi-first context** — Arabic-first where appropriate and Saudi B2B operating realities.
2. **Governed execution** — provenance, permissions, approvals and deterministic controls around sensitive actions.
3. **Revenue wedge** — a concrete first business workflow rather than a company-wide transformation promise.
4. **Proof discipline** — baseline, action, outcome and evidence are separated.
5. **Executive command** — priorities, owners, decisions, blockers and next actions are compressed into an accountable command layer.
6. **Integration posture** — use existing company tools/data where practical rather than demand wholesale replacement.

## Buyer-specific positioning

### Founder / General Manager
Dealix turns fragmented company signals into a governed list of priorities, decisions and next actions, with proof before expansion.

### Revenue / Commercial leader
Dealix helps expose leakage, follow-up gaps, stalled opportunities and weak evidence, then turns them into governed actions and measurable next steps.

### Operations
Dealix gives workflows explicit owners, boundaries, acceptance criteria and outcome records rather than adding an unowned AI layer.

### Finance / Procurement
Dealix begins with a bounded, customer-specific scope and explicit evidence/exit criteria rather than an open-ended transformation claim.

### IT / Security
Dealix should be evaluated through defined data/integration boundaries, least privilege, controlled execution and auditability—not by assuming unrestricted agent access.

### Legal / Governance
Dealix uses source/provenance, consent/suppression where applicable, approvals and evidence boundaries. Exact compliance claims remain scoped to verified evidence.

## Decision-defensibility message

A buyer should be able to answer internally:
- Why this problem now?
- Why Dealix rather than another point tool or manual process?
- What exact workflow is in scope?
- What data/access is required?
- Who remains accountable?
- What can the AI do, and what remains gated?
- How will value be measured?
- What happens if the Pilot does not justify expansion?

If the brand asset cannot help answer these questions, it is not ready for a serious B2B buying group.

## Current entry path

Use current commercial authority at publication time. The present path is:

`Free Mini Diagnostic -> Qualified Discovery -> Customer-Specific Quote -> 30-Day Revenue Command Pilot -> Proof -> Stop/Expand/Redesign`

No public first-launch fixed price or self-serve checkout is implied.

## Compliance language

Do not state blanket `PDPL-compliant`, `fully compliant`, `certified`, or equivalent claims without exact evidence covering the scope claimed.

Preferred phrasing is scoped and factual, for example:
- Saudi-first and PDPL-aware
- designed around consent, provenance and approval controls
- governed data/action boundaries
- ZATCA-aware where the exact workflow makes that relevant
