# Dealix Enterprise Asset System V1

Status: **DRAFT / MASTERBRAND-SUBORDINATE / INTERNAL EXECUTION**  
Date: 2026-09-09

This document turns the Dealix masterbrand into reusable B2B decision assets. It is subordinate to the canonical platform, commercial, claims, proof and brand authorities. It does not create a new brand, CRM, proof system, procurement qualification, consent or external-action authority.

## 1. Why this exists

Saudi enterprise buying is not won by a logo alone. A serious buyer, procurement team, partner or portfolio company needs a compact set of defensible materials that answer:

- Who is Dealix?
- What exact problem can it execute?
- What is proven versus unproven?
- How will it integrate?
- Who owns risk and acceptance?
- What data, security and governance boundaries apply?
- What is the commercial route?
- Can the supplier/partner be evaluated without inventing facts?

The asset system therefore converts the same masterbrand into decision-ready evidence for different buying groups.

## 2. External-market evidence behind the system

### PIF MUSAHAMA
PIF describes MUSAHAMA as a unified supplier-intelligence platform intended to increase local-supplier visibility and connect suppliers with potential procurement opportunities and portals across more than 150 PIF portfolio companies.

Source: https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/private-sector-hub/private-sector-initiatives/musahama-program/musahama-platform/

Implication for Dealix: supplier visibility must be backed by structured capability, category, local-content and evidence materials rather than a generic marketing brochure.

### PIF Supplier Development Program
PIF’s Supplier Development Program includes more than 40 targeted measures spanning demand transparency, supplier capability/capacity and competitiveness.

Source: https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/private-sector-hub/private-sector-initiatives/musahama-program/supplier-development-program/

Implication for Dealix: every supplier-facing asset should support capability assessment, delivery readiness and improvement—not only persuasion.

### Aramco supplier model
Aramco states that suppliers must register and qualify according to supplier type/location, and that registration/qualification does not guarantee future business.

Source: https://www.aramco.com/en/what-we-do/suppliers/become-a-supplier

Implication for Dealix: `REGISTERED`, `QUALIFIED`, `INVITED`, `BID`, `AWARDED` and `CUSTOMER` must remain distinct truth states.

### Saudi national design-system direction
The Digital Government Authority describes the national design system as a reference for consistency, reusable components and digital inclusivity.

Source: https://dga.gov.sa/ar/digital-knowledge/national-design-system-of-Saudi-Arabia

Implication for Dealix: Arabic, accessibility and component consistency are first-class quality requirements even though Dealix must not copy government branding.

## 3. One masterbrand, six decision lenses

Dealix remains one masterbrand. The evidence is adapted—not the identity.

| Buying group | Primary question | Asset emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Founder / GM | Why now, what moves economically, how much founder time? | Executive Diagnostic, decision brief, outcome hypothesis |
| Revenue / Commercial | Where is leakage and how does execution change? | Revenue workflow map, action plan, proof metrics |
| Operations | Who owns what and what will change in the workflow? | operating blueprint, RACI, acceptance criteria |
| Finance / Procurement | What is bounded, reversible and commercially defensible? | commercial summary, assumptions, exclusions, milestones |
| IT / Security | What touches systems/data and how is it controlled? | architecture, integration boundary, security appendix |
| Legal / Governance | What are consent, provenance, approval and evidence boundaries? | governance appendix, data/action boundary, claim/proof class |

## 4. Canonical asset stack

### A. Company Profile
Purpose: explain category, operating model, capabilities and evidence boundaries in 8–12 pages.

Required sections:
1. Dealix identity and promise
2. business problem
3. Signal → Decision → Action → Proof
4. Revenue + Proof + Command wedge
5. capability families
6. Build / Configure / Integrate / Partner / Subcontract / Refer routes
7. Saudi-market operating context
8. governance and proof model
9. delivery method
10. CTA: one broken workflow / execution diagnostic

Blocked:
- historical fixed public prices;
- fake customer logo wall;
- unsupported market-leadership;
- generic “we automate everything” copy.

### B. One-Page Capability Statement
Purpose: fast internal forwarding by a champion or procurement contact.

Required fields:
- Dealix category
- 3–5 capability families maximum
- representative executable problems
- delivery routes
- governance/proof boundary
- verified company/contact facts
- QR / link to diagnostic or evidence pack

### C. Execution Diagnostic
Purpose: convert a broad business pain into an evidence-backed problem definition.

Required blocks:
- workflow / decision
- owner
- tools/data
- failure/leakage evidence
- economic impact hypothesis
- baseline
- target
- proof metric
- urgency/trigger
- evidence gaps
- next verification action

A diagnostic is not automatically a qualified opportunity, consent, quote, invoice or customer proof.

### D. Customer-Specific Proposal
Purpose: make a bounded decision easy to defend internally.

Required blocks:
- why this problem / why now
- verified context
- problem statement
- evidence / unknown split
- proposed route: BUILD / CONFIGURE / INTEGRATE / PARTNER / SUBCONTRACT / REFER
- scope / exclusions
- acceptance criteria
- implementation stages
- dependencies
- risk / rollback
- governance boundaries
- commercial assumptions
- proof plan
- decision required

### E. Weekly Proof Pack
Purpose: keep delivery accountable and expansion evidence-based.

Required blocks:
- committed outcome
- work completed
- proof class
- before/current evidence
- acceptance status
- unresolved risks
- customer decision needed
- next week
- reusable learning / asset created

### F. Final Outcome Review
Purpose: distinguish delivery activity from accepted value.

Required blocks:
- original problem
- baseline
- delivered scope
- accepted evidence
- achieved / not achieved / unknown
- customer acceptance
- permission state for public proof
- expansion / stop / redesign decision

## 5. Dealix Supplier Readiness Passport

This is a reusable truth-backed pack that can support supplier portals, partner discussions and enterprise procurement without claiming registration or qualification that has not occurred.

### Identity and corporate facts
- legal entity name — exact current evidence only
- commercial registration — evidence only
- VAT / tax status — evidence only
- national address — evidence only
- banking letter/details — only in controlled procurement context
- ownership / authorized signatory — evidence only
- founder/company contact points

### Capability taxonomy
- AI Business Operating System
- governed AI / agentic execution
- revenue/commercial workflows
- data/decision/RAG
- integrations / automation
- operational command / proof
- AI governance support
- customer-specific solution delivery

Only categories that are actually supportable should be selected in a portal.

### Delivery readiness
- delivery method
- technical architecture patterns
- project cell / ownership
- acceptance method
- issue/risk process
- rollback/reversibility
- support model

### Security / data / governance
- security posture summary
- least-privilege approach
- data residency/boundary statement when evidenced
- secrets handling
- audit/provenance
- consent/suppression boundaries
- incident/escalation route
- third-party/subprocessor disclosure where applicable

Do not claim certifications that do not exist.

### Local content / Saudi operating context
- Saudi presence / operating context — exact facts only
- Saudi talent or subcontracting — exact facts only
- localization approach
- Arabic support
- partner network — only verified relationships
- local-content status/score — only if formally evidenced

### References and proof
- internal capability proof
- runtime/production proof
- customer delivery proof
- customer outcome proof

Do not use a reference client without exact evidence and publication/use permission.

### Financial / capacity facts
- turnover, staff count, insurance, guarantees, bank capacity or similar fields must be `UNKNOWN_NOT_EVIDENCE_BACKED` until exact evidence exists.

## 6. Trust & Security Appendix

A reusable appendix should answer:

1. system boundary
2. data categories
3. authentication/authorization
4. least privilege
5. secrets management
6. logging/auditability
7. retention/deletion
8. consent/suppression
9. third parties
10. model/data boundary
11. human approval boundaries
12. incident response
13. backup/recovery where relevant
14. production-change authority
15. known gaps / non-certifications

The appendix must distinguish:
- implemented control;
- tested control;
- production control;
- planned control;
- certification/attestation.

## 7. RFP / Tender Decision Pack

Before any bid commitment, create a pack containing:

- authority / buyer
- tender ID and current source
- deadline(s)
- exact scope
- mandatory eligibility
- reference requirements
- financial/guarantee requirements
- cybersecurity/data requirements
- local-content requirements
- required registrations/classifications
- partner/subcontract options
- Dealix capability match
- execution capacity
- evidence gaps
- GO / PARTNER / SUBCONTRACT / NO-BID recommendation
- founder/material approvals required

`Tender publication != Dealix eligibility` and `supplier registration != tender invitation` remain mandatory Truth Firewall rules.

## 8. Partner / Subcontractor Pack

Use when Dealix can create value but should not prime-contract alone.

Required:
- opportunity and buyer problem
- exact Dealix work package
- partner-required work package
- boundary of customer relationship
- technical dependencies
- evidence ownership
- pricing/contracting assumptions — draft until authority
- data/security boundary
- delivery acceptance
- anti-bypass / relationship handling only when legally/commercially agreed

Never claim a partner relationship before it exists.

## 9. Event / Meeting System

### Pre-meeting brief
- company
- role
- verified trigger
- why them / why now
- 3 questions
- one relevant capability
- one evidence artifact
- one next step

### Same-day follow-up pack
- what was actually discussed
- agreed problem / unknown
- requested artifact
- diagnostic or proof link
- next decision/date if actually agreed

`Event presence != buyer intent`.

## 10. Digital contact surfaces

### Founder Office email
Use for individually researched, relevant, evidence-backed B2B contact and known/warm/inbound situations under current channel authority.

### Founder Business Line / WhatsApp
Use for inbound, warm, referred or otherwise eligible conversations. A public number is not consent. No cold WhatsApp blasts.

### Website
The canonical website path should move the visitor toward:

`problem → evidence → diagnostic → qualified discovery → customer-specific decision`

not toward a fake self-serve checkout or unsupported ROI claim.

## 13. Quality gates

An enterprise asset is not READY unless:

- masterbrand tokens are current;
- Arabic/English direction is correct;
- public claims have evidence/source references;
- proof class is explicit where material;
- no historical price or unsupported customer claim appears;
- buying-group question is answered;
- CTA is one clear next action;
- source and template version are recorded;
- no personal/secret/procurement data is exposed unintentionally;
- accessibility basics are met;
- human review is required before external material action.
