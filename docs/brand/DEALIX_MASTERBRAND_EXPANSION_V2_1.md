## 0. Mission

Evolve Dealix from a good startup identity into a complete Saudi-first enterprise technology masterbrand without fragmenting the current Company OS, commercial truth, or proof model.

This is an **expansion of the existing Dealix masterbrand**, not a competing brand, product family, agency identity, or campaign theme.

The goal is a system that can remain coherent across:

- public website and product UI;
- Arabic and English;
- company profile, proposals, tenders and proof packs;
- executive dashboards and command-room views;
- founder and company social channels;
- video, motion and event environments;
- partner/co-branded materials;
- developer documentation and technical architecture;
- trust, governance, compliance and security surfaces;
- future SaaS/product packaging.

The visual identity should make Dealix feel **credible before it feels futuristic** and **operational before it feels decorative**.

## 1. Founder-approved visual direction

The 2026-09-09 founder reference establishes the desired direction:

- bright, premium, executive cloud surfaces rather than a heavy dark-only site;
- a compact geometric Dealix mark next to a strong dark wordmark;
- blue/cyan/teal signal energy with restrained green support;
- Saudi context through authentic geography, architecture, bilingual copy and business reality;
- product intelligence shown as an integrated operating interface, not generic AI artwork;
- one large clear system narrative from signal to measurable action/proof;
- premium whitespace, glass/translucent surfaces and structured information density;
- dark command surfaces used as contrast moments rather than the entire identity.

### Important truth correction for generated/reference visuals

Reference renders may contain placeholder organizations, metrics, names or dashboards. They are **visual-direction references only**.

Production Dealix assets must never publish:

- customer logos unless a current permission/evidence record exists;
- Saudi government logos or Vision 2030 marks merely as decoration;
- fabricated pipeline/revenue/ROI numbers;
- fake testimonials or executives;
- synthetic dashboard values presented as customer outcomes;
- blanket compliance claims.

Where a concept dashboard is used, label it `CONCEPT`, `DEMO`, or `ILLUSTRATIVE` unless backed by current production/customer proof.

## 4. Logo system — expansion contract

### 4.1 Founder reference mark

The preferred founder visual direction uses a **faceted forward-signal mark** with blue/teal/green geometry and a strong `Dealix` wordmark.

The mark should be refined into a vector master before replacing every canonical file. The vector must preserve the founder reference silhouette, not become a new unrelated symbol.

Working conceptual name:

**Signal Fold / Forward D**

Meaning:

- folded planes = fragmented signals becoming one operating path;
- forward geometry = decision → action;
- open negative space = governance boundary;
- blue/cyan = intelligence and signal;
- teal/green = execution and value realization.

### 4.2 Required lockups

1. Primary horizontal — mark + `Dealix`.
2. Compact horizontal — mark + wordmark, no descriptor.
3. Mark-only — favicon/app/social avatar.
4. Arabic support lockup — mark + `Dealix` + authored Arabic support copy where appropriate.
5. Reversed — white/cloud wordmark on Ink Navy.
6. Monochrome black.
7. Monochrome white.
8. Motion logo — controlled signal/fold reveal; not required for recognition.

### 4.3 Non-negotiable logo tests

Before canonical replacement:

- silhouette recognizable at 16, 24 and 32 px;
- works without gradient;
- one-color black and white pass;
- light/dark/background-photo pass;
- legible at navbar and document-header sizes;
- no visual collision with Saudi government marks or major direct competitors;
- no descriptor inside the icon;
- no fake 3D detail that disappears at small sizes;
- SVG is source of truth; raster exports are derived only.

### 4.4 Clear space

Use at least `0.5 × mark height` around the horizontal lockup and `0.25 × mark height` for constrained UI chrome. No text or partner mark enters that zone.

## 5. Color architecture

The current V2 core remains the authority. V2.1 expands it into usable tonal scales rather than inventing a competing palette.

### 5.1 Core colors

- Ink Navy `#0F172A` — executive trust / dark command surfaces.
- Deep Teal `#164E63` — system structure / intelligence.
- Signal Cyan `#22D3EE` — active execution / CTA / signal.
- Cloud `#F8FAFC` — light primary canvas.
- Proof Gold `#D4AF37` — verified proof/premium accent only.

### 5.2 Visual direction colors

Derived support colors may be used for illustrations and charts, not as new masterbrand authorities:

- Signal Sky — lighter cyan/blue range for cloud gradients.
- Deep Signal — navy/teal bridge for hero/product surfaces.
- Execution Mint — restrained green used for positive operational states, never as the logo’s only identity.
- Sandstone Neutral — Saudi-landscape support neutral for photography/illustration, never a government imitation.

### 5.3 Surface modes

**Cloud Executive Mode** — primary website / proposals / company profile.

- Cloud canvas.
- Ink typography.
- translucent blue/cyan panels.
- restrained signal gradient.
- high whitespace.

**Command Room Mode** — product dashboards / trust / executive analysis / hero contrast.

- Ink Navy canvas.
- Cloud text.
- Signal Cyan active paths.
- Deep Teal structure.
- Proof Gold only for verified outcomes.

### 5.4 Gradient rule

Gradients are atmospheric and directional, never semantic data encoding.

Recommended families:

- `Signal`: Deep Teal → Signal Cyan.
- `Cloud Signal`: transparent Cyan → Cloud.
- `Command`: Ink Navy → Deep Teal.

Never use a gradient as the only indicator of chart meaning, status, or compliance.

## 6. Typography system

### 6.1 Arabic

Primary: **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**.

This aligns with Dealix’s current V2 authority and is also used by Saudi Arabia’s National Design System as a UI typeface reference.

Rules:

- Arabic must be authored, not literal machine mirroring of English.
- Arabic headings may be slightly tighter vertically but must preserve comfortable line height.
- Mixed Arabic/English product terms should be deliberately formatted, not allowed to break bidi layout.
- Arabic numerals and Latin numerals can be selected by context; financial/technical tables should favor consistency.

### 6.2 Latin

Primary: **Inter**.

Use for display, UI, documents and sales collateral.

### 6.3 Technical/evidence

- IBM Plex Mono preferred.
- JetBrains Mono acceptable fallback.

Use only for evidence IDs, SHAs, API examples, metrics, proof classes and command-line context.

### 6.4 Type scale

Use semantic levels rather than arbitrary sizes:

- Display XL — category/hero.
- Display L — major page thesis.
- H1/H2/H3 — content structure.
- Body L/M/S — reading.
- Label — UI actions and metadata.
- Evidence — proof IDs/technical notes.

Desktop hero should remain bold and spacious; mobile must reduce scale rather than wrap into oversized blocks.

## 7. Grid, spacing and geometry

### Grid

- 12 columns desktop.
- 8 columns tablet.
- 4 columns mobile.
- max content width should be consistent across public pages.

### Spacing

Use an 8px foundation with 4px half-step where needed.

Core scale:

`4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 40 / 48 / 64 / 80 / 96 / 128`

### Radius

- small controls: 8–10px;
- cards: 14–20px;
- major glass/product frames: 24–32px;
- pill only for compact status/actions.

Rounded surfaces should feel engineered, not bubbly.

## 8. Motion system

Motion communicates execution state and direction. It is not decoration.

### 8.1 Two motion classes

**Productive motion**

Use for:

- button feedback;
- state changes;
- navigation;
- card expansion;
- data loading;
- signal routing.

Characteristics: fast, subtle, low-amplitude.

**Expressive motion**

Use sparingly for:

- hero entry;
- logo reveal;
- major proof completion;
- product demonstration transitions.

### 8.2 Signature motion

The Dealix motion grammar is a **forward signal**:

`signal appears → converges → decision locks → action moves → proof resolves`

This can drive:

- hero micro-animation;
- slide transitions;
- video bumpers;
- dashboard state changes;
- event screen loops.

### 8.3 Accessibility

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

Reduced-motion mode should:

- remove parallax;
- remove continuous peripheral motion;
- replace large translations/zoom with fades;
- preserve status through text/icon/color, not animation alone.

## 12. Data visualization

Charts are a core brand surface because Dealix sells decision clarity and proof.

### Rules

- rich neutrals first;
- categorical colors use clearly separated hues;
- sequential data use one controlled scale;
- status colors are semantic, not brand alternatives;
- Proof Gold can highlight one verified result, not entire dashboards;
- always pair color with label/shape/position where practical;
- no fake dashboard metrics in marketing.

### Proof visualization

Every result visualization should distinguish:

- baseline;
- measured period;
- result;
- evidence source;
- proof class;
- confidence/unknowns where relevant.

## 21. Accessibility and bilingual quality gates

Target WCAG 2.2 AA as the operating baseline for public/product interfaces unless a stricter requirement applies.

Required checks:

- color contrast;
- focus visibility;
- keyboard navigation;
- alt text / meaningful icons;
- reduced motion;
- 200% zoom resilience;
- RTL layout;
- mixed bidi text;
- touch target size;
- charts not dependent on color alone;
- Arabic content authored and reviewed separately.

Saudi Digital Government design guidance is a useful quality benchmark for bilingual accessibility, but Dealix must maintain its own private-sector identity rather than imitate a government interface.

## 25. Legal / trademark gate

Current Dealix brand authority already states `LEGAL_CLEARANCE_PENDING`.

External research also identifies live third-party uses of the word `DEALIX`, including a long-standing U.S. registration in business/advertising services and a current `dealix.ai` fundraising product. This does **not** determine Saudi registrability or final legal risk by itself, but it makes legal clearance a real pre-scale gate.

Before large paid media, expensive signage, trademark enforcement, international expansion or irreversible rebranding spend:

1. run Saudi trademark search/class review through SAIP;
2. review target GCC/international jurisdictions and relevant Nice classes;
3. document word-mark and logo-mark clearance separately;
4. decide whether masterbrand, mark, descriptor or category language needs adjustment.

Do not claim `registered`, `globally cleared`, or exclusive rights without current evidence.

Dealix identity is considered mature when:

- one canonical logo family exists;
- one machine-readable token system exists;
- website/product/docs/social/events all look recognizably Dealix;
- Arabic is first-class and not visually secondary;
