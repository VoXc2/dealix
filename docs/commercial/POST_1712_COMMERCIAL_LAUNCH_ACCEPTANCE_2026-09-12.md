# Dealix Post-#1712 Commercial Launch Acceptance

Mode: internal execution contract, draft-only external effects
Base: `main@98c3ab4f85483a1f6131c3a92c50cb6d06900a60`

## 1. Commercial objective
Turn the merged Company Machine into verified economic movement without creating a second CRM, second scheduler, fake pipeline, or public fixed-price catalog.

Priority function: `expected verified economic movement / founder minutes / cost / risk`.

## 2. Truth gates
- Research != relationship.
- Public contact != consent.
- Draft != sent.
- Qualified problem precedes a customer-specific proposal unless the customer explicitly requests one.
- Quote != invoice; invoice != payment; payment != revenue.
- Delivery != customer value; customer value != public proof.
- All Dealix diagnostic depths remain FREE and card-free.
- Quantified ROI requires a documented customer/source basis; otherwise mark UNKNOWN or ESTIMATED.

## 3. Commercial funnel acceptance
`INBOUND_OR_OPT_IN_SIGNAL -> REAL_INTERACTION -> QUALIFIED_PROBLEM -> FREE_DIAGNOSTIC -> QUALIFIED_DISCOVERY -> CUSTOMER_SPECIFIC_PROPOSAL -> DECISION -> VERIFIED_PAYMENT -> DELIVERY -> CUSTOMER_VALUE -> PROOF_PERMISSION -> EXPANSION`

Every transition must preserve source, actor, timestamp, authority, evidence reference, next action and stop/kill condition.

## 4. Money-Now ordering
1. Existing reciprocal commercial relationships with a concrete next step.
2. Existing inbound/opt-in Dealix diagnostic requests.
3. Warm sent threads only when a bounded follow-up is evidence-appropriate.
4. Official Saudi market signals only as diagnostic/content hypotheses; never auto-promote to relationship or pipeline.

Founder-income collaborations stay separate from Dealix company revenue accounting.

## 5. Website / inbound acceptance
- Arabic-first with English parity and correct RTL/LTR behavior.
- One primary CTA: start the Free Execution Diagnostic.
- No card, payment, CRM credential, mailbox credential or WhatsApp credential required for the free diagnostic.
- Sector pages derive from the canonical sector registry; no disconnected microsites.
- Every sector page states buyer/problem hypotheses as hypotheses until customer evidence exists.
- No fabricated customer logos, testimonials, certification claims, government-access claims or guaranteed ROI.
- Conversion events record source/UTM, consent provenance, sector, buyer role, diagnostic route and next-action state without creating revenue truth.
- Form failure, validation failure and CRM/queue handoff failure must be observable.

## 6. Diagnostic acceptance
- One universal diagnostic engine with sector and buyer-role overlays.
- All depths FREE.
- Outputs use truth classes: OBSERVED, CUSTOMER_PROVIDED, PATTERN, ESTIMATED, UNKNOWN, RECOMMENDED.
- Diagnostic output contains: problem frame, evidence gaps, baseline questions, risk, safe next step and stop condition.
- No proposal or price generated from research-only evidence.

## 7. Discovery / proposal acceptance
Discovery must resolve current workflow, owner, evidence, baseline, constraints, urgency, integrations, decision process, data boundary and acceptance criteria.

Proposal factory must produce customer-specific: problem, evidence, scope, exclusions, implementation plan, integrations, acceptance criteria, timeline, risks, dependencies, customer responsibilities, measurement, proof permission and commercial placeholders.

No public fixed pricing. Price/terms remain founder-approved commercial authority.

## 8. Negotiation acceptance
For a real relationship, maintain: counterpart objective, Dealix objective, confirmed terms, unknowns, objections, alternatives, concession ladder, non-price trades, walk-away conditions, next ask and exact unsent payload.

Never use fake scarcity, invented authority, fabricated competitive bids or unsupported performance claims.

## 9. Delivery / proof acceptance
Authorized work must create baseline -> scope -> tasks -> dependencies -> acceptance -> evidence -> customer validation -> proof-permission states.

Public proof is forbidden until explicit permission exists. Internal proof must distinguish code readiness, delivery evidence, customer value and verified payment.

## 10. Saudi commercial wedges
Use official signals only as evidence-backed entry points:
- Fatoora readiness / operational integration readiness.
- AI adoption, AI-agent governance and reliability readiness.
- B2G supplier/tender readiness using official procurement evidence.
- Open Banking partner/readiness opportunities subject to licensed-provider boundaries.
- Sector-specific workflow, compliance, operations and revenue diagnostics from canonical packs.

Signal receipts never count as buyer demand.

## 11. Channel law
- WhatsApp: inbound/opt-in only; no cold outbound.
- Email: prepare bounded drafts; live send requires exact external-send authority.
- LinkedIn personal: human/native interaction; no scraping or autonomous personal-account outreach.
- Content: draft internally; public publish requires exact publish authority.

## 12. Daily autonomous commercial cycle
1. Reconcile Gmail/inbound/CRM/company-state evidence.
2. Score real interactions by closeability, time-to-cash, evidence strength, founder minutes and risk.
3. Select <=3 deep commercial actions.
4. Generate/refresh free diagnostic, discovery prep, proposal input, negotiation packet or delivery action as appropriate.
5. Persist receipts to the canonical Company Machine.
6. Put only exact L5 actions into Approval Queue.
7. Run truth/consent/revenue gates.
8. Produce compact MONEY / DECISIONS / RISKS / APPROVALS / NEXT_ACTION brief.

## 15. Definition of Done
Commercial launch is GREEN only when:
- deployed identity matches the approved release;
- the free diagnostic path works end-to-end and is observable;
- inbound evidence reaches the canonical queue without synthetic promotion;
- discovery/proposal/delivery/proof factories pass truth gates;
- one compact approval queue contains only genuine material actions;
- founder time is spent on exceptions and material decisions, not routine operation;
- revenue remains zero unless payment evidence proves otherwise.
