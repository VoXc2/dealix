## Content operating contract

Every reusable asset should have:

- stable `content_id`;
- source evidence references;
- facts vs inferences vs unknowns;
- pillar and buying-group role;
- core thesis and CTA;
- brand version and claim version;
- target channel and format;
- publish class and status;
- UTM metadata where clickable;
- permission/approval refs where relevant;
- provider receipt and published URL only after real execution;
- outcome references and business metrics.

No evidence means refine/reuse/stop, not fabrication.
