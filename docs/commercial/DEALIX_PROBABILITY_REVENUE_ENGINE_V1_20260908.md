# Dealix Probability Revenue Engine V1

Date: 2026-09-08 Asia/Riyadh

## Purpose

Increase the number of useful market attempts without turning Dealix into a spam engine or a founder-operated research desk.

The scalable interpretation of "play the probabilities" is:

- maximize **signal discovery, verification, enrichment and qualification attempts**;
- keep deep commercial work concentrated on the highest expected-value opportunities;
- use real outcomes to update allocation;
- stop immediately on suppression, opt-out, channel ineligibility, weak fit or unacceptable risk;
- never infer external-send authority from volume, score, public contact details or AI recommendation.

## Canonical loop

`Economic Signal -> Verify -> Account -> Buying Committee -> Pain Hypothesis -> Evidence/Unknowns -> EV Score -> Top-3 -> Diagnostic -> ChannelEligibility -> Founder Draft -> Real Interaction -> Qualified Problem -> Discovery -> Route -> Quote -> Verified Payment -> Project Cell -> Delivery -> Customer Acceptance -> Permissioned Proof -> Learning`

## Capacity design

The contract deliberately creates a wide top-of-funnel and narrow execution WIP:

- up to 1,500 raw signal refreshes/day;
- up to 300 verified relevant signals/day;
- up to 75 account hypotheses/day;
- up to 30 buying-committee maps/day;
- up to 15 personalized diagnostic drafts/day;
- at most 3 deep commercial accounts at once;
- at most 1 material action packet at once.

These are capacity ceilings, not claims that the market contains that many valid opportunities every day.

## Expected value

The engine prioritizes opportunities using known evidence only:

`EV = P(real problem) * P(reach decision maker) * P(response | eligible channel) * P(qualified discovery) * P(close) * Value / CostRisk`

Where Value includes expected margin, proof value, partner leverage and repeatability; CostRisk includes founder minutes, delivery risk, compliance risk and collection risk.

Unknown probabilities remain `UNKNOWN_NOT_EVIDENCE_BACKED` until real observations exist.

## Adaptive allocation

Initial allocation uses 20% exploration / 80% exploitation only after a minimum real-observation floor is met. Before that, allocation remains exploration-heavy and evidence gathering dominates.

Arms may include:

- sector/economic motion;
- offer;
- source family;
- buyer role;
- message angle;
- eligible channel;
- prime vs partner route.

No bandit/score may override suppression, opt-out, ChannelEligibility, legal/contract gates or the Truth Firewall.

## Attempt ladder

1. Refresh account trigger.
2. Enrich buying committee.
3. Prepare Founder email draft #1.
4. Prepare a follow-up only when new evidence supports it.
5. Search warm introduction / partner route.
6. Prepare manual Founder LinkedIn route where appropriate.
7. Prepare call/WhatsApp only when the channel is eligible.
8. Recycle to nurture/research if timing is wrong.

The engine prepares options; it does not convert a public email/phone number into consent or dispatch authority.

## Stop-loss

Downgrade or stop when:

- suppressed / opted out;
- channel is ineligible;
- evidence shows no fit;
- there is no meaningful new information for another attempt;
- the delivery route is not credible;
- expected founder minutes exceed expected economic value;
- compliance or contract risk dominates expected value.

## Learning

Every outcome must improve the machine. Store reusable learnings such as:

- targeting rule;
- objection pattern;
- eligibility gap;
- partner requirement;
- missing capability;
- offer adjustment;
- delivery lesson;
- proof pattern.

Only real outcomes update commercial probability: reply, qualified problem, discovery, quote request, verified payment, delivery acceptance and permissioned customer proof.

## Authority

`AUTONOMY_LEVEL=4`, `MODE=draft-only`.

The following remain false in this contract: external send, public publish, spend, payment execution, Production mutation, DNS, DB, secret and identity mutation.

This engine is a decision and preparation layer inside the existing Company Machine. It is not a second CRM, scheduler, Company Brain, agent fleet, consent authority or outbound platform.
