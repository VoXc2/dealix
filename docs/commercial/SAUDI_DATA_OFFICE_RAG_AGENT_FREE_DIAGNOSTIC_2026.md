## NDMO / NDI grounding

SDAIA describes the National Data Index (NDI / "نضيء") as a results-based framework for tracking government entities' progress in data-management maturity, compliance with national data-management controls/specifications, and operational excellence. SDAIA states that the maturity component spans **14 data-management domains** and that NDI also measures compliance and operational indicators.

Official sources:
- https://sdaia.gov.sa/ar/Research/Pages/NationalDataIndex.aspx
- https://sdaia.gov.sa/ar/Sectors/Ndmo/Pages/default.aspx

## Free diagnostic framework

### A. Data-office operating model
- named executive owner and Data Management Office mandate;
- decision rights, escalation and approval path;
- data-domain ownership and stewardship structure;
- RACI across business, data, IT, security, privacy and vendors;
- operating cadence, backlog, KPIs and evidence retention.

### B. Data inventory, ownership and provenance
- authoritative systems/sources and named owners;
- critical data domains and business use;
- lineage/provenance from source to report/model/agent output;
- freshness and version authority;
- duplicate/conflicting definitions and reconciliation rules;
- retention, archival and deletion boundaries.

### C. Data quality and evidence
- completeness, accuracy, consistency, timeliness and uniqueness checks;
- issue ownership and remediation workflow;
- quality thresholds tied to actual business decisions;
- evidence that controls operate, not only that policies exist;
- explicit `UNKNOWN` when quality cannot be proven.

### D. Warehouse / platform readiness
- current warehouse/lake/lakehouse architecture and workload boundaries;
- ingestion, transformation and orchestration ownership;
- environment separation and release identity;
- observability, failure detection and retry/idempotence;
- backup/restore and rollback evidence;
- cost and capacity authority.

### E. Governance, privacy and access
- classification of each source/output;
- least-privilege and privileged-access boundaries;
- personal/sensitive-data handling and approved processing purpose;
- third-party/cloud/model processing boundary;
- auditability of access and changes;
- unresolved legal/regulatory requirements => `HOLD`, not an assumed compliance claim.

### F. RAG readiness
- approved corpus and source-of-truth hierarchy;
- chunking/indexing only after source ownership and freshness are proven;
- Arabic/English retrieval requirements;
- metadata, citation/provenance and document-version controls;
- retrieval evaluation set and answer-grounding criteria;
- hallucination / stale-source failure handling;
- access controls preserved through retrieval;
- no secret/customer-sensitive material in uncontrolled prompts.

### G. AI-agent readiness
For every proposed agent workflow define:
1. exact business objective and baseline;
2. deterministic steps before model-driven steps;
3. trusted `DATA_SENSITIVITY` classification;
4. allowed tools and canonical sources;
5. actions allowed without a human;
6. actions requiring explicit human approval;
7. actions always denied;
8. model/provider/data/cost authority;
9. failure/timeout/retry/idempotence behavior;
10. independent verification and immutable receipts;
11. rollback / kill path;
12. measurable acceptance criteria.

A prompt, worker, environment variable, provider default or API key cannot mint data, model, cost or action authority.

### H. NDI/NDMO evidence-readiness mapping
Use the current official NDI/NDMO material only as an evidence checklist. Do not issue an official score.

For each applicable domain record:
- required policy/control/process;
- accountable owner;
- operating evidence available;
- evidence freshness;
- gap / remediation owner;
- dependency on an official NDMO interpretation or customer legal/privacy authority.

Classify evidence:
- `EVIDENCED`
- `PARTIAL`
- `MISSING`
- `NOT_APPLICABLE_PENDING_VALIDATION`
- `REQUIRES_OFFICIAL_CONFIRMATION`

## Readiness scoring

Internal diagnostic aid only:
- `0 = UNKNOWN / NO_USABLE_EVIDENCE`
- `1 = AD_HOC / MATERIAL_GAPS`
- `2 = DEFINED / PARTIAL_OPERATING_EVIDENCE`
- `3 = OPERATING / EVIDENCE_BACKED`

Score domains independently: operating model; ownership/stewardship; quality; platform/warehouse; governance/privacy/access; RAG; agent authority; observability/continuity; evidence/NDI readiness.

Never average away a blocker. Restricted-data uncertainty, missing authority, unproven rollback/restore, unresolved official requirement, weak source provenance, or unsafe agent action authority keeps the recommendation at HOLD.
