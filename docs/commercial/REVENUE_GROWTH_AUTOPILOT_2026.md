## 2. Revenue operating architecture

### Layer A — Market Intelligence

Continuously research permitted public/authorized sources for:
- Saudi/GCC B2B accounts;
- event participants;
- startup/founder ecosystems;
- tenders/programs;
- hiring/expansion/funding/product-launch signals;
- partner/co-delivery candidates;
- competitor/category changes;
- buyer questions and objections.

Outputs remain `RESEARCH_ONLY` until real evidence upgrades state.

### Layer B — Account / Buying Group Intelligence

For each high-value account:
- economic buyer;
- revenue/business owner;
- technical/security buyer;
- finance/legal/procurement hidden buyers;
- actual users/operators;
- likely current-system owner;
- decision criteria;
- adoption risk;
- proof required by each persona.

No inferred persona is presented as fact without source/evidence.

### Layer C — Relationship Engine

Allowed evidence-bearing relationship triggers:
- inbound request/reply;
- event conversation;
- referral/introduction;
- explicit opt-in;
- existing business relationship;
- native-network interaction with a real two-way response.

Public directory listing, scraped contact, profile view, like, or CRM import alone is not a verified relationship.

### Layer D — Diagnostic / Discovery

The system prepares:
- account evidence brief;
- problem hypotheses;
- diagnostic questions;
- current process map;
- leakage/risk hypotheses;
- required evidence list;
- buying-group map;
- smallest measurable Pilot hypothesis.

AI prepares; the customer supplies facts. Unknown stays unknown.

### Layer E — Proposal Factory

Proposal inputs:
- customer-stated problem;
- current-state evidence;
- target outcome;
- scope boundary;
- data/integration assumptions;
- approval/governance needs;
- success measurement;
- explicit exclusions;
- delivery plan;
- customer-specific commercial terms.

Never use a generic fixed-price proposal as authoritative when current policy is quote-only.

### Layer F — Negotiation Desk

Negotiation objective is not maximum price at all costs. Optimize:

`EXPECTED VERIFIED VALUE = win_probability * contribution_margin * proof_value * expansion_value * strategic_value - delivery_risk - founder_load - collection_risk`

Every negotiation records:
- counterpart position;
- evidence supporting their constraint;
- our BATNA;
- their likely BATNA as hypothesis;
- reservation boundary;
- ideal outcome;
- tradable variables;
- non-tradable authority/legal/security boundaries;
- concessions already used;
- next ask;
- next proof required.

Trade variables before price:
- scope;
- timeline;
- access/data availability;
- implementation responsibility;
- payment timing;
- milestone structure;
- support level;
- proof/case-study permission;
- referral/introduction;
- renewal/expansion option;
- exclusivity only with explicit strategic/legal review.

Concession rule:
`NEVER GIVE; ALWAYS TRADE.`

Each concession should request a reciprocal movement, e.g. smaller scope for faster decision, discount only for reduced scope/prepayment/longer commitment/case permission when commercially appropriate and specifically approved.

No AI may commit to named price, contract, legal term, payment/refund, exclusivity, warranty, tender or other sensitive commitment without the required authority gate.

### Layer G — Delivery / Proof / Expansion

A won Pilot becomes:
- delivery plan;
- weekly evidence receipts;
- outcome measurements;
- proof gaps;
- final Proof Pack;
- renewal/expand/stop decision;
- permissioned case/reference/referral asset.

Only verified delivered outcomes feed public/customer proof.

## 6. Negotiation playbook

### Discovery before bargaining

Never negotiate price before enough context exists to understand:
- cost of current problem;
- urgency/deadline;
- decision process;
- alternatives;
- budget mechanism rather than guessed budget number;
- technical/security dependencies;
- success criteria;
- hidden buyer objections.

### Objection classes

`PRICE`
`TIMING`
`PRIORITY`
`TRUST`
`SECURITY_DATA`
`INTEGRATION`
`INTERNAL_BUILD`
`COMPETITOR`
`NO_BUDGET`
`NO_AUTHORITY`
`NO_URGENCY`
`PROCUREMENT_LEGAL`
`UNKNOWN`

Response sequence:
`ACKNOWLEDGE -> CLARIFY -> EVIDENCE -> REFRAME -> OPTION -> CONFIRM NEXT STEP`

Do not fabricate case studies, benchmarks, compliance status or competitor claims.

### Negotiation options hierarchy

1. preserve value and scope;
2. reduce scope before discounting;
3. phase delivery;
4. change payment/milestone structure within approved authority;
5. exchange concession for reciprocal value;
6. walk away if economics/trust/risk fail threshold.

### Walk-away triggers

- unlawful/unsafe use;
- request to fabricate claims/proof;
- unacceptable payment/collection risk;
- security/data requirements Dealix cannot truthfully satisfy;
- scope fundamentally outside current delivery capability;
- margin/founder-load below approved floor;
- customer requires guarantee Dealix cannot support;
- abusive or bad-faith negotiation.
