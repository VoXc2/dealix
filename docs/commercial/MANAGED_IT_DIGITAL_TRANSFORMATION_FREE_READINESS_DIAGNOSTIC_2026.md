## Diagnostic objective

Produce a decision-grade view of whether an organization or delivery partner is ready to run a managed-IT / digital-transformation scope with explicit ownership, evidence, resilience and governed automation — and identify the smallest credible improvement path.

This diagnostic is vendor-neutral and may be used for public-sector, enterprise, SI/MSP or partner contexts. It is not certification, audit opinion, legal advice, tax advice, a tender compliance declaration or proof that a customer has a problem.

## Evidence requested

Ask only for evidence appropriate to the authorized engagement. Do not request secrets in chat.

1. **Service inventory and criticality** — services, applications, infrastructure, integrations, owners, dependencies and recovery priorities.
2. **Operating model** — service desk, incident/request/problem/change process, escalations, approved SLAs/SLOs, backlog and recurring manual bottlenecks.
3. **Observability and evidence** — monitoring coverage, alert quality, logs/metrics/events, change/incident trail and actual outcome evidence.
4. **Continuity and recovery** — backup inventory, restore evidence, rollback/release identity and documented RTO/RPO where applicable.
5. **Security / data / access** — identity, privileged access, data classes, secret handling, supplier/model/cloud boundaries and entity-specific requirements.
6. **Digital-transformation execution** — target outcome, workflow/system of record, automation candidates, integrations, acceptance and rollback.
7. **AI / agent readiness (conditional)** — decision value, deterministic/model boundary, data sensitivity, authority matrix, failure mode and safe fallback.
8. **Supplier / partner delivery readiness** — responsibility split, evidenced references, staffing/escalation, subcontract boundaries and commercial dependencies.

## Readiness score

Score each domain 0–3 from evidence only:
- **0 — Unknown / no evidence**
- **1 — Ad hoc / owner or control unclear**
- **2 — Defined / partial evidence / material gaps remain**
- **3 — Operating / evidence-backed / acceptance and rollback explicit**

Domains: service ownership; ITSM/operating process; observability/evidence; continuity/restore; security/data/identity; integration/automation; delivery governance/acceptance; supplier/partner readiness.

Do not convert this score into a compliance grade or public claim.

## Stop / hold rules

Return **HOLD** when any material prerequisite is unresolved:
- owner / authority cannot be identified;
- production data class or processing boundary is unknown;
- rollback / restore is required but unproven;
- mandatory legal / procurement / classification evidence is missing;
- partner role would overlap or misrepresent another provider;
- required references, guarantees, staffing or working-capital commitments are unproven;
- proposed automation needs a material external effect without exact action-bound authority;
- expected value cannot be distinguished from activity volume.
