## 4. Channel model

### Website / Search
Owned discovery and conversion surface. Every important page maps a real buyer problem to a supported capability and a single next action.

### Founder LinkedIn
Manual/native trust surface. Internal systems may research, draft, rank and recommend. No automated LinkedIn messaging, connections, scraping or engagement.

### Email
Eligible relationship/nurture surface. A discovered email address alone is never permission.

### WhatsApp
Relationship continuation surface, not a cold-acquisition blast channel. Documented opt-in/prior interaction/inbound or equivalent eligibility is required.

### Big 5 / LEAP / DeepFest
Relationship acquisition surfaces. The goal is evidence-rich conversations, not scanned badges or imported exhibitors counted as pipeline.

### Partnerships
A leverage surface for market access, implementation, referrals and B2G routes where Dealix lacks direct eligibility or proof.

## 5. Website conversion architecture

Primary journeys:

```text
Founder insight -> Dealix website -> Free Mini Diagnostic -> Discovery
Event conversation -> event QR -> event landing -> Diagnostic -> Discovery
Organic search -> problem page -> evidence/framework -> Diagnostic
Partner introduction -> partner one-pager -> partner discovery
```

Mandatory qualities:
- mobile-first;
- Arabic/English parity;
- one dominant CTA per page;
- clear source/UTM capture;
- no unsupported logos, customer outcomes or public first-launch prices;
- fast, crawlable pages with canonical metadata and internal links.

## 6. SEO + generative search

Treat AI-search visibility as an extension of strong SEO, not a separate hack.

Build around:
- crawl/index health;
- Search Console;
- unique Saudi expert experience;
- non-commodity implementation content;
- real diagrams/screenshots/examples when safe;
- clear sections/headings;
- useful Arabic and English topic clusters;
- page experience;
- supported structured data only when it matches visible content.

Do not rely on fake AI schema, keyword stuffing, inauthentic mentions, mass commodity AI pages, or `llms.txt` as a Google-ranking tactic.

## 7. Event-to-revenue operating loop

### Pre-event
1. Research universe.
2. Rank by ICP, pain, buyability and relationship potential.
3. Build hall/time route.
4. Prepare one-line problem hypothesis + three questions per priority target.
5. Prepare QR diagnostic landing and one-pagers.

### During event
Capture exactly what happened: person/company/role, location/time, discussion, pain in their words, follow-up permission/preference and explicit next step.

### Same day
Run the evidence through Revenue Mesh and Deal Desk. Produce a channel-eligible follow-up/diagnostic action without inflating pipeline.

### 24–72 hours
Move only evidence-backed opportunities to diagnostic/discovery. Create a customer-specific proposal after sufficient discovery.

## 9. Attribution

Track from source to money, not just impressions.

Core events:
`qualified_visit -> diagnostic_start -> diagnostic_submit -> real_interaction -> verified_relationship -> qualified_problem -> discovery_booked -> discovery_completed -> proposal_sent -> pilot_agreed -> payment_verified -> delivery_proof -> referral -> expansion`

Primary KPIs are relationships, qualified problems, diagnostics, discovery/proposal/pilot conversion, time-to-paid-pilot, verified revenue and proof/referral/expansion.
