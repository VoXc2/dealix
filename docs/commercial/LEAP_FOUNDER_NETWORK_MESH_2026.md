## 1. Mission

Use LEAP x DeepFest and the wider Saudi startup ecosystem as a **founder-to-founder relationship engine**, not a scraped lead list.

Canonical chain:

`STARTUP UNIVERSE -> SOURCE-BOUND PROFILE -> FOUNDER HYPOTHESIS -> HUMAN/NATIVE INTERACTION -> VERIFIED RELATIONSHIP -> CUSTOMER / PARTNER / REFERRAL / COMMUNITY LANE -> DIAGNOSTIC OR PARTNER DISCOVERY -> PILOT / CO-DELIVERY / REFERRAL -> PROOF`

This implementation reuses the canonical Marketing & Distribution OS, Revenue Mesh, Portfolio Control Plane, Campaign Acceptance, Opportunity Graph, Approval Center and Proof Ledger. It creates no parallel CRM, scheduler, agent fleet or outreach engine.

## 3. Founder priority lanes

### F0 — Inbound first
Any real LEAP Connect request, reply, referral or introduction is handled before cold research. It is a relationship **candidate**, not a verified relationship, until two-way evidence exists.

### F1 — Rocket Fuel 100
Research all finalists as a high-signal cohort. Founder time is reserved for the highest-fit subset: Saudi relevance, B2B pain, complementarity, partnership potential and real accessibility.

### F2 — Saudi / Saudi-market-active founders
Prioritize B2B SaaS, enterprise software, AI/deep tech, professional-services technology, fintech infrastructure, healthtech B2B, logistics/manufacturing tech, smart infrastructure, edtech/talent, cybersecurity and governed AI.

### F3 — Founder Pass and Startup Pods
Use Founder Directory, matchmaking, curated meetups, Startup Pods and founder spaces as human/native relationship surfaces. Presence in a directory is not consent for bulk marketing.

### F4 — DeepFest AI founders
Main opportunities: integration, co-delivery, referral, governed AI implementation, shared enterprise accounts and operator knowledge exchange.

### F5 — Wider Saudi startup ecosystem
Extend through accelerators, VC portfolios, Monsha'at/NTDP ecosystem surfaces, event alumni and referrals from verified founder relationships.

## 4. Founder-to-founder value exchange

Do not open with a generic pitch.

Use:

`WHY YOU -> WHAT I NOTICED -> FOUNDER-LEVEL QUESTION -> SHARED PROBLEM / COMPLEMENTARITY -> SMALL MUTUAL NEXT STEP`

High-value paths:

1. Saudi market-entry lessons.
2. Revenue/operating bottleneck comparison.
3. Relevant cross-introductions.
4. Product/integration partnership.
5. Co-delivery / implementation partnership.
6. Joint workshop or diagnostic for a shared account with explicit roles.
7. Founder content/event recap after mutual permission.
8. Proof-methodology exchange.
9. Dealix Mini Diagnostic only after the founder acknowledges a relevant problem.
10. Referral loop after trust exists.

## 5. Relationship truth model

State machine:

`RESEARCH_ONLY -> IDENTIFIED -> MEETING_CANDIDATE -> INTERACTED -> TWO_WAY -> VERIFIED_RELATIONSHIP -> COLLABORATION_DISCOVERY -> CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY / ACTIVE_PARTNER / REFERRAL / COMMUNITY -> DORMANT / CLOSED`

Hard rules:

- directory listing != lead
- public founder name != permission
- LEAP Connect profile != verified relationship
- connection acceptance != qualified opportunity
- research score cannot promote relationship state
- event badge/card/scan needs provenance and follow-up context
- proposal != revenue
- payment evidence is required for revenue

## 6. Founder attention score

Score only for attention allocation, never qualification authority:

- Founder accessibility / event presence: 15
- Saudi relevance / market-entry intent: 15
- Dealix complementarity: 15
- Shared B2B operating pain: 15
- Referral network potential: 10
- Partner/co-delivery potential: 10
- Proof/content collaboration potential: 5
- Current trigger / Rocket Fuel / launch / funding signal: 10
- Real relationship evidence: 5

Hard truth/consent gates override score.

## 7. Channel strategy

### LEAP Connect
Primary event-native discovery/matchmaking surface. Automate research, ranking, meeting briefs, draft preparation, evidence capture and next-action queues. Keep actual native messages/meeting requests manual unless LEAP exposes an authorized workflow/API for them.

### In person
Highest-trust path. Before approach: read a one-page founder/startup brief. During conversation: ask one founder-level question, capture actual pain/complementarity, ask permission for follow-up, and record the next step.

### LinkedIn
**MANUAL_NATIVE** for personal connections/messages/comments. Internal research/ranking/drafting is allowed. No scraping, bots, auto-connect or automated DMs.

### Email
Use only when sourcing and relationship/consent policy permit. A public email address alone is not permission.

### WhatsApp
Only for documented opt-in, real relationship, inbound request or equivalent channel eligibility. No cold/bulk founder blast.

## 8. Event operating rhythm

### Before LEAP
- Cover the complete public Startup & Scaleup universe as `RESEARCH_ONLY`.
- Prioritize Rocket Fuel 100, Saudi/MENA B2B + AI, Saudi soft-landing founders, complementary providers and partner candidates.
- Prepare one-page founder briefs for the top cohort.
- Prepare questions and mutual-value hypotheses, not generic proposals.
- Prepare Dealix founder bio, QR, one-liner and event visual assets.

### Each morning / pre-open
Generate a physically feasible top-20:
- 5 must-meet
- 5 partner candidates
- 5 customer-fit candidates
- 5 ecosystem/referral candidates

### During the event
Capture:
- person/startup/role
- booth/location/time
- what was actually discussed
- their words for pain/goal
- permission/preferred follow-up channel
- next step
- source/confidence

### Within 15 minutes after a strong interaction
- update evidence/relationship state
- produce founder/account brief
- classify customer/partner/referral/community lane
- draft contextual follow-up
- prepare discovery questions
- list evidence gaps
- set exact next action

### Same evening
- founder relationship ledger
- highest-value new relationships
- partner/customer/referral routing
- sanitized content insights
- next-day reranking

### 4-18 Sep
- 24h follow-up for permissioned high-value interactions
- 72h second touch only with new value
- 7-day relationship review
- convert real pain into diagnostic/discovery
- no repetitive cadence without new evidence/value

English:

> I'm the founder of Dealix in Riyadh. I noticed you're building in <specific area>. I'm building a Saudi-first AI Business OS around Revenue + Proof + Command. Rather than pitch you, I'm curious: what's the hardest operating/revenue bottleneck you are facing as you scale? If there is a real overlap, we can compare notes or test a small collaboration.

Post-real-interaction:

> Great meeting you at LEAP. The one point I wrote down was <their actual point>. I can send a short 1-page diagnostic/idea around that if useful. No generic deck.

## 12. Definition of Done

- Full official startup universe can be researched with provenance and defaults to `RESEARCH_ONLY`.
- Rocket Fuel 100 is a distinct high-signal cohort.
- Founder identity always has source + confidence.
- No directory or social profile becomes relationship without two-way evidence.
- Founder time is allocated to feasible top cohorts, not 700+ indiscriminate contacts.
- LEAP Connect and in-person are primary event-native channels.
- LinkedIn personal activity stays manual-native.
- No cold/bulk WhatsApp.
- Every real founder conversation has next-evidence + next-action state.
- Dealix distinguishes CUSTOMER / PARTNER / REFERRAL / COMMUNITY outcomes.
- Success is verified relationships, discoveries, partner/referral paths, paid pilots and proof—not connection count.
