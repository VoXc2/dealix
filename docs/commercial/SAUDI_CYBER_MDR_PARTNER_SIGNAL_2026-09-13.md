## Free MDR / cyber-operations readiness diagnostic

Use as a bounded diagnostic framework for eligible customers/partners. Do not call it an NCA audit or certification.

### 1. Service ownership
- named service owner and escalation path;
- in-scope assets, environments and business criticality;
- 24x7 operating expectations and service boundaries;
- customer/provider responsibility matrix.

### 2. Telemetry and observability
- log-source inventory and coverage;
- collection health and freshness evidence;
- alert quality / false-positive burden;
- evidence retention and traceability;
- visibility gaps that prevent reliable detection.

### 3. Detection and response
- priority threat/use-case coverage;
- triage ownership and time-to-decision;
- containment authority boundaries;
- incident evidence chain;
- human approval points for consequential actions.

### 4. Resilience and continuity
- SOC/platform outage fallback;
- communication escalation;
- runbooks and manual recovery;
- backup/restore evidence where relevant;
- tested continuity rather than documentation-only claims.

### 5. Identity, data and third parties
- privileged access boundaries;
- secret handling;
- data classes and processing locations;
- third-party responsibilities;
- customer-specific regulatory constraints.

### 6. Governed automation / AI readiness
- deterministic tasks before model-driven tasks;
- prompt/tool injection boundaries;
- model/data/provider authority;
- explicit approval gates for external or destructive actions;
- independent verification and immutable receipts;
- fail-closed path when evidence or authority is missing.
