# Dealix — Saudi Market Evidence Register

Observed / reverified: 2026-09-07

Purpose: provide current official Saudi anchors for Market-to-Delivery strategy. These sources establish market context, supplier/procurement routes and regulatory constraints. They **do not** prove buyer intent, Dealix eligibility, customer consent, a contract, or revenue.

## 1. Digital market context

### CST — ICT market
Official source: https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/N2026070101

Observed fact:
- Saudi Arabia ranked first in ITU ICT Development Index 2026.
- CST states the Saudi ICT market reached **SAR 199 billion by end-2025**.
- CST reports **8% CAGR over the previous five years**.

Dealix implication:
- Sufficient market depth exists for technology implementation and managed operations.
- This is **not** Dealix TAM/SAM/SOM and cannot be used as buyer-demand proof for an individual company.

## 2. Digital economy context

### GASTAT — Digital Economy Survey 2024
Official source: https://stats.gov.sa/en/News

Observed fact:
- GASTAT reported the digital economy represented **16.0% of Saudi GDP in 2024**, up from 15.6% in 2023.
- It reported ICT-sector operating revenues of SAR 249.8 billion in 2024.

Dealix implication:
- Digital work spans well beyond chatbot/AI-only projects.
- Do not collapse economy-wide measures into Dealix obtainable revenue.

## 3. SME AI adoption / operating demand signal

### Monsha'at + SDAIA — AI Week
Official source: https://www.monshaat.gov.sa/en/node/502808

Observed fact:
- AI Week ran 9–13 August 2026.
- More than **3,900 beneficiaries**, 40+ entities and **78 networking meetings** were reported.
- Focus included putting AI to work in products, services and business operations.

Dealix implication:
- Position around operational execution and measurable outcomes rather than generic “AI agents”.
- Event participation or directory presence remains research signal, not relationship or consent.

## 4. Industrial transformation route

### SIDF — Future Factories / Tanafusiya
Official sources:
- https://www.sidf.gov.sa/Initiatives/Industrial-Sector-Support-Initiative/Future-Factories
- https://www.sidf.gov.sa/FinancialSolutions/Incentive-Programs/Tanafusiya

Observed fact:
- Tanafusiya supports qualifying factories adopting **automation, digitization and energy-efficiency** projects.
- Current requirements include commercially proven technologies, economic feasibility and feasibility/capex/opex evidence.

Dealix implication:
- Manufacturing wedge should emphasize measurable operational/admin workflows and commercially proven technology.
- Shop-floor control/safety work is partner/domain-expert first unless Dealix has qualified capability and authority.

## 5. Supplier / large-enterprise route

### PIF — MUSAHAMA
Official source: https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/private-sector-hub/private-sector-initiatives/musahama-program/musahama-platform/

Observed fact:
- MUSAHAMA is designed to strengthen local supplier visibility and procurement engagement.
- It connects suppliers to **150+ PIF portfolio companies** and their procurement/supplier portals.

Dealix implication:
- Build supplier readiness, capability evidence and procurement routing as a parallel enterprise-access lane.
- Registration/visibility is not a contract or buyer intent.

## 6. Partnership / investment matchmaking route

### MISA / Invest Saudi — Investors Matchmaking
Official source: https://investsaudi.sa/

Observed fact:
- Investors Matchmaking provides Partner Access, Opportunity Listing and Partnership Matching.
- It is intended to connect registered companies/investors and partnership opportunities.

Dealix implication:
- Useful for partner and market-access research, particularly foreign vendors or complementary delivery capacity.
- A listed company or matching result is not a verified partnership.

## 7. Government procurement data route

### Etimad Developer Portal
Official sources:
- https://apiportal.etimad.sa/en/docs/introduction
- https://apiportal.etimad.sa/ar/docs/subscriptions
- https://portal.etimad.sa/ar-sa/services/servicedetails?ServiceGuid=ab8b780f-d6ab-48a3-9bfa-a3a073791d73

Observed fact:
- Etimad provides an official Developer Portal/API subscription model.
- API access is linked to registered applications/products, rate plans, sector relevance and approval/terms.

Dealix implication:
- Prefer official, authorized procurement interfaces over brittle scraping where access is appropriate.
- API availability does not create tender submission authority or entitlement to all data/products.

## 8. AI / cybersecurity governance context

### NCA — AI Cybersecurity Guidelines
Official source: https://nca.gov.sa/en/news/2354/

Observed fact:
- NCA’s 2026 AI Cybersecurity Guidelines consultation covers governance, defense, resilience and third-party cybersecurity.
- Scope explicitly includes **generative AI and agentic AI**.

Dealix implication:
- “Governed execution + proof” is strategically stronger than autonomous-agent novelty.
- Customer deployments need identity, access, auditability, third-party/subprocessor and resilience controls.

### NCA — provider registration/licensing
Official source: https://nca.gov.sa/en/registration-and-licensing/

Observed fact:
- Registration is a regulatory requirement for entities providing cybersecurity products/solutions/services in the Kingdom.
- Managed SOC services have separate licensing tiers/requirements.

Dealix implication:
- Specialized cybersecurity work must be eligibility-checked and, where required, delivered via qualified/registered/licensed partners.
- Never infer that generic software/AI capability authorizes every cybersecurity service.

## 9. Financial-sector outsourcing boundary

### SAMA — Rules on Outsourcing
Official source: https://rulebook.sama.gov.sa/en/rules-outsourcing

Observed fact:
- Banks remain responsible for regulatory/legal compliance when outsourcing.
- **Material outsourcing requires written SAMA no-objection**.
- Rules address due diligence, data confidentiality, subcontracting and exit/control provisions.

Dealix implication:
- Initial finance wedge should favor bounded administrative/technical modules and qualified partner routes.
- Do not market Dealix as automatically eligible for bank material outsourcing.

## Strategic conclusion

The official evidence supports four high-level theses:

1. Saudi digital/ICT demand is large enough to justify a multi-capability implementation company.
2. Industrial transformation is specifically tied to measurable digitization/automation and economic feasibility.
3. Enterprise/public routes increasingly reward supplier readiness, qualified partnership and official procurement pathways.
4. AI adoption must be paired with governance, security, evidence and third-party controls.

Therefore the Dealix market doctrine remains:

`BROAD RADAR -> VERIFIED PROBLEM -> BEST-FIT SOLUTION ROUTE -> GOVERNED DELIVERY -> CUSTOMER-VALIDATED PROOF`

No source in this register grants Dealix a customer relationship, consent, procurement award, regulated-service eligibility, public claim authority or guaranteed market demand.
