# Dealix — Saudi Cybersecurity Commercial Regulatory Gate (2026)

**Status:** internal commercial/governance control  
**Evidence checkpoint:** 2026-09-13  
**Authority:** research + internal qualification only; not legal advice, not a registration/license claim, not tender authority

## Why this gate exists

Current official National Cybersecurity Authority (NCA) material states that entities providing cybersecurity services, solutions or products in the Kingdom are required to register. NCA also maintains a separate licensing framework for Managed Security Operations Center (Managed SOC) services, including level-specific scope.

Official evidence:
- NCA registration and licensing hub: https://nca.gov.sa/ar/registration-and-licensing/
- NCA provider registration service: https://nca.gov.sa/ar/services/cybersecurity-services-providers-registration/
- NCA provider licensing service: https://nca.gov.sa/ar/services/cybersecurity-services-providers-licensing/
- NCA registered entities list: https://nca.gov.sa/ar/registration-and-licensing/entity-list/

At the 2026-09-13 checkpoint, the NCA registration page states that all entities providing cybersecurity services, solutions or products should register. The same hub states that Managed SOC services are subject to a dedicated licensing framework; level 1 covers all entities including government/CNI contexts, while level 2 excludes government and CNI contexts. NCA material also states that level-2 applications remain open.

These facts do **not** establish that Dealix currently requires a particular registration/license for every diagnostic, that Dealix is eligible for a license, or that a specific customer engagement is regulated in a particular way. Applicability must be classified from the actual scope before a paid cyber service is offered or delivered.

## Commercial gate

Before any customer-specific quote, contract, tender, paid pilot or delivery that could reasonably be a cybersecurity service/solution/product, classify the scope:

1. **Business/AI readiness diagnostic only** — determine whether the work is general operational/AI readiness or materially a cybersecurity professional service.
2. **Cybersecurity advisory / assessment / implementation** — verify NCA provider registration applicability and any activity-specific conditions before external commitment.
3. **Managed SOC / MDR / monitoring / incident-response operations** — HOLD until the applicable NCA licensing level, service boundaries, eligibility, subcontract/partner model and customer class are verified.
4. **Government/CNI context** — apply the strictest evidence/eligibility gate; never infer that level-2 or general registration is sufficient.
5. **Partner-delivered service** — verify the partner's current registration/license, exact licensed entity name, scope and responsibility split; public listing != partnership.

## Dealix entry strategy

Use cyber demand primarily to create **free, evidence-based readiness diagnostics** and partner qualification until the applicable registration/licensing boundary is proven.

Reusable free diagnostics may include:
- NCA control-to-evidence readiness;
- AI/agent cybersecurity governance readiness;
- MDR/SOC operating-model readiness;
- incident-response evidence/handoff readiness;
- supplier/partner cyber delivery readiness.

A diagnostic must not claim certification, compliance, license coverage, penetration-test authority, SOC/MDR delivery authority or guaranteed risk reduction.

## Required evidence before monetization

- precise proposed scope and deliverables;
- whether the activity is a cybersecurity service/solution/product under current NCA rules;
- Dealix registration/license status if applicable;
- partner registration/license and permitted scope if partner-led;
- customer class (government, private, CNI or other);
- data/systems access boundary;
- professional/legal/contract requirements where applicable;
- staffing and delivery competence;
- customer-specific acceptance criteria;
- economics and liability exposure;
- exact action-bound L5 authority for any external commitment.

## Truth firewall

`registration requirement != Dealix registration`  
`registered provider != licensed Managed SOC provider`  
`provider listing != partner relationship`  
`readiness diagnostic != certification`  
`diagnostic != permission to operate security systems`  
`research != legal opinion`  
`draft != sent`  
`quote != contract`  
`delivery != customer-validated value`
