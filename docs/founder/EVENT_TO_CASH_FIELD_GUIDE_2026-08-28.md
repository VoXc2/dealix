## Qualification after a real answer

A record may move from `RESEARCH_ONLY` to a relationship/opportunity state only if the interaction produces evidence.

Capture:

- person + role
- company
- event + timestamp
- exact pain/goal in their words
- current workflow/tools
- who owns the decision
- data/evidence availability
- urgency/timing
- permission/preferred follow-up channel
- next step

### Good first-pilot indicators

- Pain is specific, current and expensive enough to matter.
- One owner can sponsor a 30-day bounded workflow.
- There is a baseline or data source.
- The workflow crosses handoffs/approvals/next actions where Dealix can add command + proof.
- Buyer is willing to share enough evidence for a Mini Diagnostic/Discovery.

### Hold indicators

- General interest in AI only.
- No owner.
- No identifiable workflow.
- No evidence/data boundary.
- Procurement-only contact with no business sponsor.
- Asking for broad transformation before a bounded proof.
