## 1. Purpose

Compress the annual strategy into a parallel execution system that discovers, validates, monetizes, proves, productizes, and scales the highest-value Saudi opportunities without compressing truth, production trust, customer evidence, compliance, or L5 authority.

This is not permission to launch every arm deeply at once. It creates a broad **portfolio of experiments and revenue surfaces**, then allocates deep WIP only to evidence-backed winners.

## 2. Compression law

`EXPLORE_BROADLY -> VALIDATE_CHEAPLY -> SELL_BEFORE_BUILD -> DELIVER_GOVERNED -> PROVE -> PRODUCTIZE -> SCALE`

- Broad opportunity research may run in parallel.
- Deep WIP remains <= 3.
- No arm receives product-engineering capacity without customer, buyer, tender, partner, or usage evidence.
- Every arm must have a measurable value event and kill condition.
- No arm may create a duplicate CRM, scheduler, proof ledger, Company Brain, model router, or permanent agent.
- L5 remains exact-action-bound.

## 6. Tier A — execute/validate immediately

### A1. Revenue Command Pilot

**Buyer:** Saudi SME/mid-market founder, GM, commercial director, sales leader.

**Problem:** fragmented CRM/email/WhatsApp/files, weak follow-up, unclear pipeline truth, no executive command loop.

**Offer:** diagnostic -> discovery -> customer-specific quote -> 30-day governed pilot.

**Outcome:** one operating layer for opportunity prioritization, follow-up drafts, approval queue, proof pack and executive command.

**Revenue:** setup/pilot + recurring Revenue Command Room retainer.

**Productization path:** repeatable workflows -> private SaaS modules.

### A2. Revenue Leak Diagnostic / Execution Diagnostic

**Buyer:** founder/CEO/GM/head of sales.

**Problem:** money leaking between lead, response, qualification, proposal, follow-up, payment, delivery.

**Offer:** rapid evidence-based diagnostic with quantified workflow gaps where evidence permits.

**Revenue:** free/low-friction entry -> paid sprint/pilot.

### A5. Saudi Market Access Sprint

Alternative terminal states:

## 15. Kill conditions

Kill/pause an arm when two or more are true after a bounded test:

- no credible buyer signal;
- no access path to buyer;
- no willingness-to-pay evidence;
- delivery requires excessive founder time;
- low gross margin with no strategic moat;
- duplicates stronger existing product/vendor;
- legal/compliance cost dominates value;
- requires fake proof/unsupported claim/spam;
- cannot be integrated into one Company OS;
- does not create reusable data/process/policy/proof asset.

## 16. Capital allocation rule

Until verified cash and Production Green:

- 35% Production Trust / engineering
- 30% Revenue / commercial movement
- 15% Delivery / proof readiness
- 10% productization of only proven workflows
- 5% security/data/compliance
- 5% R&D/tool harvesting

No speculative arm may consume more than a small bounded experiment budget before real signal.
