# Dealix — Saudi Market Evidence Ledger

**Evidence date:** 2026-09-10
**Purpose:** primary-source market evidence supporting the fast-compression portfolio.

## Truth rules

- A public market signal is not buyer intent.
- An event attendee/exhibitor is not a lead or relationship.
- A government tender is demand evidence, not Dealix eligibility or authority to bid.
- An investment opportunity is ecosystem evidence, not an instruction for Dealix to enter capital-intensive manufacturing.
- Every commercial promotion must pass the canonical Opportunity Graph and commercial authority ladder.

## Digital / AI market

### CST — ICT market and digital infrastructure

Source: https://www.cst.gov.sa/media-center/news/N2026070101

Evidence:
- Saudi Arabia ranked first globally in the ITU ICT Development Index 2026.
- CST states the communications and technology market reached approximately SAR 199B by end-2025.
- CST states five-year CAGR was approximately 8%.

Dealix implication:
- Strong base for an AI Business Operating System overlay, but market size is not Dealix TAM proof by itself.

### CST — Saudi Internet Report 2025

Source: https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/knowledge-center/reports/saudi-internet-report-25-dashboard

Evidence:
- 99.6% internet penetration.
- 45.2% adoption of AI tools, more than double prior year.
- 61.3% of internet users spend seven or more hours online daily.
- 53GB average monthly mobile data use per person.
- 216 Mbps median mobile download speed.

Dealix implication:
- AI awareness/distribution friction is falling.
- The commercial gap is increasingly governed execution, integration, proof, and economics rather than basic AI awareness.

### CST — Cloud Computing Market Maturity Index

Source: https://www.cst.gov.sa/en/media-center/news/N2026090303

Evidence:
- Index launched 2026-09-03.
- Measures service diversity, technical expertise, customer satisfaction, strategic partnerships, service delivery efficiency, and sales/marketing across 33 sub-indicators.

Dealix implication:
- Build a Cloud/AI readiness and evidence layer through partners; do not become a commodity CSP.

## Market-to-product rules

Promote a market signal into product work only when all applicable evidence exists:

1. named buyer/problem pattern;
2. repeated signal or strong one-off budget evidence;
3. Dealix delivery fit;
4. bounded measurable outcome;
5. lawful data/interaction path;
6. acceptable delivery and compliance risk;
7. reusable process/data/policy/proof asset;
8. positive founder-minute economics.

Otherwise keep it `RESEARCH_ONLY` or `WATCH`.
