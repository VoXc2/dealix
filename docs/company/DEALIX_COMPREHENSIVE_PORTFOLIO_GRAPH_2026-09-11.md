# Dealix Ω∞ — Comprehensive Portfolio Graph

Effective: 2026-09-11

## Doctrine

> Dealix should have an extremely wide economic radar, an extremely large executable capability surface, and an extremely narrow active bottleneck.

Operational meaning:

- research and addressability may span hundreds of capability cells;
- addressability never becomes relationship, opportunity, pipeline or revenue by itself;
- exactly five permanent agents remain canonical;
- Company OS, Company Brain, Opportunity Graph, Approval Authority, Proof Ledger, Economic Model, Scheduler and Model Router remain singular;
- Deep WIP remains capped at three evidence-qualified moves;
- every promotion is evidence-gated and every material L5 effect remains exact-action-bound.

## Addressable capability formula

`SECTOR × BUYER × PROBLEM × ARM × FACTORY × OFFER × MONETIZATION × CHANNEL × PROOF_PATH × AUTHORITY`

The result is a capability hypothesis surface, not a lead list.

## Promotion model

`DISCOVER → VALIDATE → COMMERCIAL_TEST → PROVEN_WEDGE → REPEATABLE → PRODUCTIZE → SCALE`

A candidate cannot become Deep WIP merely because it has a high numeric score. Required gates include source-bound evidence, a valid/non-blocked arm, validated problem, buyer-access evidence, delivery feasibility, channel/legal authority where applicable, and no suppression block.

## Economic dispatcher

Positive dimensions:

`evidence_strength, urgency, buyer_access, economic_impact, gross_margin, automation_ratio, collection_probability, proof_reuse, strategic_reuse, expansion_value, partner_leverage, productization_potential`

Penalties:

`time_to_cash, founder_minutes, delivery_cost, compute_cost, sales_cycle, working_capital_risk, compliance_risk, security_risk, irreversibility, maintenance_debt, opportunity_cost`

The resulting score is a prioritization heuristic, not a forecast of purchase probability or booked revenue.

## Top-three operating law

At every President cycle:

1. keep the full radar visible;
2. block candidates that violate truth/authority/evidence gates;
3. keep incomplete candidates `RADAR_ONLY`;
4. rank only eligible candidates by current economic evidence;
5. promote at most three to Deep WIP;
6. spend founder attention and compute on those highest-value moves;
7. demote or stop a cell when its kill condition or stop-loss is reached.

## Sector and regulated boundaries

- Fatoora is a cross-sector technical integration/readiness/exception-operations capability; applicability must be verified and it is not tax/legal certification.
- Open Banking work must not imply Dealix is a licensed provider; regulated execution uses an appropriately qualified/licensed partner where required.
- Healthcare scope remains non-clinical administrative/interoperability unless qualified authority is independently proven.
- Cybersecurity evidence operations do not imply SOC, penetration-test or certification authority.
- Data Licensing remains `BLOCKED` until source rights, provenance, privacy review and paying demand are proven.

## Verification

Focused local acceptance for this implementation contract must prove:

- 44 arm identities;
- five permanent agents only;
- exactly three strategic `ACTIVE_DEEP` arms;
- >=500 addressable capability cells without pipeline inflation;
- no suppressed or blocked candidate promotion;
- no research-only candidate promotion;
- regulated/data-rights gates remain fail-closed;
- daily runner composes the existing Company OS and scheduler rather than creating a duplicate;
- no external send, publish, bid, payment, merge, deploy, DNS/DB/secret/identity action is performed by the President Portfolio Command.

## Authority

This implementation is L0-L4 only. It does not authorize or execute merge to `main`, production deployment, external communication, public publishing, tender submission, payment/spend/refund, binding commercial/legal commitment, or DNS/DB/secret/identity mutation.
