## 1. Purpose

Turn the governed Arm Registry into an executable portfolio without creating parallel company systems.

Every arm must move through the same economic truth chain:

`SIGNAL -> EVIDENCE -> VALIDATED_PROBLEM -> BUYER_ACCESS -> COMMERCIAL_TEST -> VERIFIED_PAYMENT -> DELIVERY -> CUSTOMER_PROOF -> REPEATABILITY -> PRODUCTIZATION -> SCALE`

The company explores broadly but invests deeply in at most three strategic wedges at once.

Current deep strategic wedges remain:

1. Revenue Command + AI Company OS
2. Governed AI Execution
3. Saudi Market Access + Partner + B2G Intelligence

All other arms are supporting, validating, watching, or blocked until their promotion evidence exists.

## 2. Non-negotiable operating law

- Exactly five permanent agents: `dealix-pm`, `dealix-sales`, `dealix-delivery`, `dealix-engineer`, `dealix-content`.
- One Company Brain, Opportunity Graph, Approval Authority, Consent/Relationship truth model, Proof Ledger, Economic Model, Scheduler, Model Router, Dev Factory and Learning Factory.
- `research != relationship`, `public_contact != consent`, `proposal_view != buying_decision`, `quote != invoice`, `invoice != payment`, `synthetic != customer_proof`, `deployment != correct_release`.
- L0-L4 may execute autonomously. L5 material effects remain exact-action-bound.
- Regulated, licensed, clinical, tax/legal-certification and safety-critical delivery is partner-first unless Dealix authority is independently proven.
- A new arm may reuse canonical capabilities; it may not create a duplicate CRM, scheduler, approval system, proof system or permanent agent fleet.

## 5. Nine economic engines and their role

### Core Cash Engine
Convert qualified business problems into verified cash and customer proof quickly. It funds everything else.

### Recurring Revenue Engine
Attach ongoing operating value to live data, proof, reliability, governance and command—not generic advisory hours.

### Productized Service Engine
Turn repeated, measurable workflows into bounded offers with explicit inputs, outputs, acceptance criteria and proof.

### Data & Intelligence Engine
Create decision-grade Saudi signals and evidence products only where provenance, rights and action utility are clear.

### Software & API Engine
Externalize internal contracts only after repeated customer/partner pull and stable semantics. No idea-led API factory.

### Partner & Channel Engine
Use reciprocal partner leverage for access, regulated/specialist delivery and distribution. Reject logo collection.

### Education / Media / IP Engine
Package only proven Dealix operating methods and customer-safe knowledge. Content supports trust and distribution; it does not substitute for proof.

### B2G & Regulated Enterprise Engine
Operate partner-first, evidence-first and qualification-aware. Supplier registration, regulated status and tender eligibility are never inferred.

### Venture & Asset Engine
Activate only after core cash/proof capacity is strong enough to absorb optional bets without founder-attention dilution.

## 7. Common arm scorecard

Every active/validating arm should report:

- fresh evidence count and source quality
- real buyer/problem access
- commercial test count
- qualified movement rate
- verified payment, when applicable
- contribution margin / delivery burden
- time to proof
- proof quality and permission state
- reuse/repeatability score
- founder minutes consumed
- next promotion gate
- kill-condition status

Vanity metrics such as raw contacts, drafts, impressions, opens or scraped leads remain diagnostic only.

## 8. Promotion and kill protocol

Promote an arm only when its next gate has explicit evidence. Do not promote because the market category looks attractive.

Pause or kill an arm when:

- the buyer problem is not repeated,
- lawful access/authority cannot be established,
- unit economics are structurally poor,
- the arm requires unsupported regulated authority,
- a mature existing tool solves the job without Dealix differentiation,
- the arm creates permanent system duplication,
- founder attention exceeds expected verified value.

Learning from a killed arm is retained in the Company Brain and Self Improvement ledger.
