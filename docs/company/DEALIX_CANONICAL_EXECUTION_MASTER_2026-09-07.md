## Delivery / Proof

Every paid project becomes a bounded Project Cell with customer, owner, outcome, scope, acceptance, tenant/data boundary, tools/provider, specialist/reviewer, budget, TTL, security/compliance profile, rollback and proof plan.

Delivery loop:

`PLAN -> BUILD/CONFIGURE/INTEGRATE -> TEST -> CUSTOMER ACCEPTANCE -> OPERATE -> MEASURE -> VERIFY -> DOCUMENT -> PROOF`

Proof answers:

- What changed?
- Compared with what baseline?
- How was it measured?
- Who verified it?
- Did the customer accept it?
- Can the result be reproduced?

Separate internal evidence, synthetic evidence, delivery acceptance and customer-validated proof.
