# DEALIX CORPORATE BRAND + GTM V1

Effective: 2026-09-10

## Corporate definition

Dealix is the parent Saudi B2B company.

Dealix is not defined by one software product. The company operates across four connected practices:

1. Strategy & Transformation
2. Systems & Automation
3. Intelligence & Market Access
4. Trust, Governance & Proof

The product layer sits inside the company. **Dealix OS** is the flagship product and keeps the category **AI Business Operating System**.

## Positioning

**Arabic:** شركة B2B سعودية تبني الاستراتيجية والأنظمة والذكاء والمنتجات لتحويل إشارات الأعمال إلى تنفيذ محكوم ونتائج قابلة للإثبات.

**English:** Saudi B2B strategy, systems, intelligence and product company turning business signals into governed execution and measurable proof.

Commercial wedge remains **Revenue + Proof + Command**.

## Website architecture

- `/` — corporate front door
- `/company` — corporate model and practices
- `/services` — strategic B2B solution portfolio
- `/dealix-os` — flagship product
- `/proof-vault` — evidence/proof model
- `/safety` — governance and authority boundaries
- `/book` — Execution Diagnostic entry point

The website should start from the buyer's business problem, not from an agent/tool catalog.

## Visual system

The canonical visual language is premium Saudi enterprise technology: deep ink surfaces, teal structural layers, cyan signal/data accents, restrained gold, high whitespace, precise grids and proof/receipt motifs.

Use IBM Plex Sans Arabic for Arabic surfaces. Use a clean Latin sans for English. Technical evidence may use IBM Plex Mono.

Avoid robot-first imagery, generic neon cyberpunk, crowded AI icon walls, unsupported leadership claims, and synthetic dashboards presented as real customer results.

## Market entry model

Primary execution lanes:

1. **Revenue Command + AI Company OS** — founder/CEO/commercial problems.
2. **Governed AI Execution** — AI owners, CIO/CTO/CDO, risk and compliance problems.
3. **Saudi Market Access + Partner/B2G Intelligence** — foreign vendors, Saudi partners and enterprise market-entry problems.

Everything else is a bounded supporting capability until evidence promotes it.

## Distribution system

Founder LinkedIn is the primary B2B thought-leadership surface. Company LinkedIn is the authority/proof surface. `dealix.me` is the owned conversion and knowledge hub. Email is permissioned/warm follow-up. WhatsApp Business is inbound/opted-in first.

X, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts are repurposing/reach channels once provider connection is proven; they do not replace the B2B conversion path.

## Content factory

One verified thesis should become channel-native assets rather than identical cross-posts:

- founder LinkedIn post
- company LinkedIn post
- short video script
- carousel outline
- X variant

Flow:

`Verified Signal -> Business Consequence -> ICP -> Thesis -> Channel Drafts -> Truth/Brand Review -> Approval -> Authorized Distribution -> Attribution -> Qualified Conversation -> Diagnostic -> Discovery -> Quote -> Verified Payment -> Proof`

## Measurement

Primary metrics are commercial and proof metrics: qualified conversations, Execution Diagnostics, Qualified Discoveries, customer-specific quotes, verified payments and customer-validated proof.

Impressions, followers and posting volume are secondary distribution signals only.

## Platform roles

- Canva: canonical Brand Kit and reusable asset production.
- Metricool: planning, best-time analytics and social measurement after networks are proven connected.
- PostHog: website/product conversion, behavior and experiment evidence.
- Ahrefs + Search Console: organic/AI visibility and search demand research when configured.
- n8n: connector/workflow bus, not a competing Company OS.
- Airtable: current Dealix operating mirror for Action Queue, Opportunity Graph, Proof and Arm Portfolio.
- GitHub: canonical product/company operating source.

## Governance

External publication defaults to draft-only. Paid amplification requires separate spend authority. Public contact is not consent. Cold WhatsApp blasts and mass LinkedIn connect/DM automation are not valid growth loops. Customer proof requires evidence and permission appropriate to the claim.
