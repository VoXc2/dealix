## Promise

Bring a qualified Saudi B2B opportunity. Dealix handles governed diagnostic, discovery,
customer-specific scope/quote, delivery and proof. Partner earnings become eligible only
from verified collected cash under the rules below.

## Motions and economics

| Motion | Services / projects | SaaS / subscription |
|---|---:|---:|
| Scout | 7.5% | 20% recurring, max 12 months |
| Growth Partner | 10% | 25% recurring, max 12 months |
| Co-Sell Partner | 12.5% | 25% recurring, max 12 months |
| Closer / Sector Partner | 15% | 30% recurring, max 12 months |
| Strategic Channel | up to 20% with margin approval | up to 30%, governed |

Rates are not stackable. One partner may perform several roles but receives one approved
motion rate unless a multi-partner attribution split is explicitly recorded.
## Net Commissionable Collected Revenue (NCCR)

Commission base is verified cash collected by Dealix, less:

- VAT and other tax collected on behalf of authorities.
- refunds, credits and chargebacks.
- pass-through advertising spend.
- third-party licenses billed at cost.
- vendor/cloud/API costs passed directly to the customer.
- government fees.

`Commission = NCCR x approved motion rate`

Quote != invoice != payment != verified collected cash.
No commission is payable merely because a deal is marked Won.

## Deal registration and attribution

- Standard protection window: 120 days.
- Evidence-backed extension: up to 180 days.
- Existing/duplicate pipeline opportunities are not commissionable by default.
- Default multi-partner pool: source 40%, qualification 20%, close 40%.
- Expansion commission applies only to incremental revenue influenced by the partner.
- Automatic lifetime renewal entitlement is not granted.
## Payout truth and clearing

- Default clearing period: 30 days after verified collection.
- Refunds/chargebacks before payout reduce or eliminate commission eligibility.
- The Partner Portal may stage an approved commission but cannot execute a bank transfer.
- `STAGED_NOT_PAID` is not Payment.
- A real payment requires an external payment executor plus immutable payment receipt.

## Hard holds

The following never auto-approve:

- government/tender opportunities before compliance review.
- government officials/employees receiving referral economics.
- compensation for real or claimed influence over a public decision.
- cold WhatsApp or unproven marketing consent.
- misleading claims, guaranteed ROI/revenue or fake proof.
- pricing, discount or binding quote promises not approved by Dealix.

## Partner tiers

V2 economics are motion-based. Legacy Bronze/Silver/Gold/Platinum tiers may remain as
portal compatibility and enablement/status surfaces, but do not override V2 economics.
Performance recognition should use verified collected revenue, conversion quality,
retention/customer quality and compliance record — not raw lead count alone.

## Regulatory research notes

Saudi PDPL implementing regulations require consent for direct marketing, clear sender identity,
an easy opt-out and evidence of consent. Saudi anti-bribery rules require strict separation
between lawful partner economics and compensation for public-sector influence. Tax treatment
is handled separately from the commission percentage according to each partner's status.
