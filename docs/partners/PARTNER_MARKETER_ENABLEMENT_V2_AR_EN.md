## Qualification card

Before registering a deal, capture only what is needed:

1. Company name/domain and Saudi B2B relevance.
2. How the partner knows the company; relationship/consent evidence.
3. Named operational pain or measurable gap — not a generic interest in AI.
4. Buyer/decision-maker access or realistic path to discovery.
5. Timing/urgency signal and any known constraints.
6. Sector and best Dealix diagnostic wedge.
7. Existing Dealix pipeline/duplicate check.
8. Government/tender flag and conflict/compliance flag.

A public email address, scraped contact or social profile is not consent and is not a relationship.

## Recommended wedge menu

- AI Operational Readiness / Governed Agents.
- FATOORA integration/readiness where evidence supports relevance.
- Hospitality AI Operations.
- Sales/Revenue Operations diagnostic.
- Workflow/automation diagnostic.
- Supplier/B2G readiness under Partner-or-No-Bid governance.
- Customer-specific sector diagnostic generated from verified evidence.

Do not promise certification, guaranteed savings, guaranteed revenue or regulatory approval.
## Objection handling

**"كم السعر؟"**  
لا يوجد سعر عام ملزم. نبدأ بـFree Execution Diagnostic ثم Qualified Discovery ثم
Customer-Specific Quote مبني على النطاق ومعايير القبول.

**"هل تضمنون النتيجة؟"**  
لا. Dealix تربط التنفيذ بالأدلة والقبول والقياس، ولا تستخدم ضمانات إيراد أو ROI مصطنعة.

**"أنا فقط مسوق ولا أفهم AI تقنيًا."**  
ابدأ Scout/Growth Partner. المطلوب فهم المشكلة وتأهيل العلاقة؛ Dealix تتولى الحل والتنفيذ.

**"هل أقدر أرسل لأي رقم واتساب؟"**  
لا. WhatsApp outbound يحتاج inbound/opt-in/consent evidence؛ cold WhatsApp ممنوع.

**"عندي شخص داخل جهة حكومية."**  
لا تسجل وعد نفوذ أو عمولة نفوذ. ضع Government/Tender flag؛ المسار يذهب Compliance Review.

## Onboarding sequence

1. Apply / identity and partner-type review.
2. Accept partner terms and compliance covenant.
3. Choose motion and sector focus.
4. Complete micro-certification: truth, consent, claims, pricing authority, B2G rules.
5. Receive referral/deal-registration access and enablement kit.
6. Register first qualified opportunity.
7. Dealix validates duplicate/relationship/compliance state.
8. Diagnostic -> discovery -> customer-specific quote -> delivery.
9. Commission ledger follows verified collections and clearing.
10. Partner performance review uses economic quality + compliance, not spam volume.
