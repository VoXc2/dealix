# Partner Program Benchmarks & Growth Research — 2026-09-17

## Research objective
Translate current partner-program patterns into Dealix without importing unsafe economics or a second authority stack.

## What strong programs currently do
- HubSpot: recurring commissions up to one year, long attribution window, application review, resource center, reporting, tiered enablement.
- HighLevel: recurring economics, training, ready-made promotional assets, affiliate portal, campaign-specific offers.
- PartnerStack: structured onboarding journeys, partner portal, resources, training, reporting, commission visibility and adjustment workflows.
- Dub: partner applications, tracked links, attribution events, fraud controls, flexible commissions, dashboards and commission analytics.

## What Dealix should adopt
1. Application review before activation.
2. Mobile-first Arabic/English self-service portal.
3. Partner IDs and tracked attribution links.
4. Transparent deal-registration and commission-state receipts.
5. Resource library with approved bilingual assets.
6. Micro-certification before activation.
7. Tier benefits based on verified economic quality and compliance.
8. Commission and attribution analytics tied to verified collected cash.
9. Fraud/refund/clawback controls.
10. Dedicated sector enablement and co-branded assets after approval.

## What Dealix must NOT copy
- No Tier-2/downline override commission.
- No commission merely for recruiting another partner.
- No guaranteed-income or guaranteed-ROI claims.
- No public fixed customer-pricing authority.
- No payout truth created from a dashboard state alone.
- No cold WhatsApp or unconsented direct marketing.
- No government-access/influence economics.

## Dealix differentiation
Dealix combines partner growth with Saudi legal classification, PDPL consent evidence, B2G holds, customer-specific quotes, NCCR economics and immutable policy/attribution receipts. Growth is measured as verified partner-sourced economic movement, not clicks or raw signups.
