# Partner Rights, Attribution & Commission Policy

## Truth Ladder
`REGISTERED → ACCEPTED_ATTRIBUTION → QUALIFIED → WON_NOT_PAID → COLLECTED → COMMISSION_ELIGIBLE → CLEARED → APPROVED_FOR_PAYMENT → STAGED_NOT_PAID → PAID_VERIFIED`

No state may be skipped without evidence.

## Deal Registration Receipt
Receipt should contain:
- registration_id
- partner_id
- company identity/domain
- normalized opportunity fingerprint
- relationship/consent evidence reference
- sector/problem
- accepted_at
- protection_expires_at
- duplicate-check result
- policy_version
- immutable content hash

## Commission Statement
For each eligible collection:
- collected cash
- VAT/tax collected on behalf of authorities
- refunds/credits/chargebacks
- pass-through ads/licenses/vendors
- government fees
- NCCR
- approved motion rate
- attribution split
- commission projected
- clearing date
- adjustments with reason/evidence

## Dispute Protection
Recommended legal drafting points:
- visible deal registration status;
- fixed dispute window after rejection/statement;
- evidence upload;
- second reviewer for material disputes;
- no silent retroactive deletion;
- immutable resolution record;
- conflicts escalated to founder/legal when threshold exceeded.

## Change Management
Policy changes should apply prospectively by default. Existing accepted registrations remain tied to the applicable policy version unless the signed agreement/law permits otherwise.

## Fraud Holds
self-referral, duplicate entity, synthetic buyer, collusion, cookie stuffing, purchased/unconsented list, forged consent, forged collection/payout proof, manipulated attribution.

## Payout
Payout remains L5/external. The internal portal can calculate and stage; bank transfer requires separate approval/executor and immutable payment receipt. `STAGED_NOT_PAID != PAID_VERIFIED`.
