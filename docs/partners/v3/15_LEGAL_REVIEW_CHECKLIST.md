# Saudi Legal / Tax Review Checklist

## Partner classification
- هل مسمى Referral Partner / Independent Sales Partner مناسب لكل motion؟
- متى تتحول العلاقة إلى Employment / Flexible Work / Remote Work؟
- ما المتطلبات المحددة لغير السعوديين حسب الإقامة/العمل/الاستثمار والنشاط؟
- هل أي motion قد يندرج تحت Commercial Agency أو regulated brokerage؟

## Contract
- governing law / jurisdiction / arbitration choice.
- notice and termination.
- effect on active registered deals after termination.
- clawback/offset enforceability and limits.
- dispute deadlines.
- e-signature identity/evidence standard.
- confidentiality/IP/non-solicit/non-compete proportionality.

## PDPL / Marketing
- lawful basis/consent form text.
- direct-marketing consent and opt-out design.
- controller/processor roles with forms, CRM, analytics, e-signature vendors.
- transfer outside Kingdom assessment for each external provider.
- retention schedule and data-subject request workflow.

## Tax / VAT
- documentation Dealix must collect from individuals vs entities.
- VAT treatment of commissions and invoice/document requirements.
- withholding or cross-border cases if ever permitted.
- ZATCA/e-invoicing implications for each partner category.

## B2G / Anti-Bribery
- success-fee restrictions and safe compensation for legitimate professional services.
- conflicts-of-interest declarations.
- gifts/hospitality.
- government employee/public official screening.
- tender-specific procurement rules.

## Advertising
- exact disclosure wording for creators/affiliates.
- review of earnings examples and disclaimer prominence.
- social platform ad compliance and competition/prize mechanics if later used.
