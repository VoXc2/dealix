# Compliance / Privacy / Claims Guard

## Saudi PDPL — Direct Marketing
Operational interpretation for Dealix:
- document consent before direct marketing where required;
- sender identity must be clear;
- opt-out must be easy and free;
- withdrawal must stop further marketing without unjustified delay;
- keep evidence of consent;
- public contact details alone are not treated as permission.

## Electronic Advertising
Before public use, content must pass:
- mark/disclose promotional nature where applicable;
- identify promoted service and service provider/contact path;
- no deceptive or false claims;
- no unauthorized trademark use;
- remember that electronic-advertisement specifications/offers can become binding.

## Claims Registry
Allowed only when evidence-backed:
- "Saudi-first" positioning if factually accurate.
- "Free Execution Diagnostic" under current authority.
- governed AI execution / evidence / approval workflows if product proof exists.

HOLD unless approved evidence exists:
- guaranteed revenue/ROI/savings.
- "certified by" regulator.
- "official government partner".
- customer logos/testimonials/results.
- exact customer pricing before customer-specific quote.

## B2G / Anti-Bribery
Hard hold if any participant:
- is public official/employee connected to decision;
- asks for success payment for influence/access;
- promises ability to obtain government award/license/order by influence;
- requests gifts/benefits linked to official action.

## Anti-Concealment
For non-Saudi independent participation, the portal must not assume eligibility. It requires a lawful authorization classification before economic activity is enabled.

## Data Minimization
Application collects only what is necessary for eligibility and operations. Sensitive ID/bank/tax documents live in restricted storage, not general CRM notes. Logs show references/hashes rather than secret contents.

## Retention / Deletion
Final legal policy must define purpose-specific retention, statutory retention, dispute holds and deletion/anonymization. Partner portal should expose privacy contact/rights process.
