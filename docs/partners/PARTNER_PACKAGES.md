# Dealix — Partner Packages V2

**Current authority:** [`PARTNER_NETWORK_V2.md`](PARTNER_NETWORK_V2.md)
**No public fixed customer pricing authority.** Customer commercial terms are created only
after Qualified Discovery as a Customer-Specific Quote.

## Scout
- Introduce a qualified non-duplicate opportunity.
- Protect attribution through Deal Registration.
- Services commission: 7.5% of eligible NCCR.
- Eligible SaaS: 20% recurring, max 12 months.

## Growth Partner
- Source + qualification evidence + responsible demand generation.
- Services commission: 10% of eligible NCCR.
- Eligible SaaS: 25% recurring, max 12 months.

## Co-Sell Partner
- Source/qualify and actively support discovery/co-selling.
- Services commission: 12.5% of eligible NCCR.
- Eligible SaaS: 25% recurring, max 12 months.

## Closer / Sector Partner
- Adds sector expertise and material closing support.
- Services commission: 15% of eligible NCCR.
- Eligible SaaS: 30% recurring, max 12 months.

## Strategic Channel
- Agency/integrator/consultancy motion with explicit commercial agreement.
- Services: up to 20% only after margin approval.
- Eligible SaaS: up to 30% under governed terms.
- White-label/reseller economics require separate delivery/legal review.

## Universal rules
- Rates do not stack automatically.
- Quote/Invoice/Won are not Payment.
- Commission base is NCCR after allowed deductions.
- Default clearing period is 30 days after verified collection.
- Government/tender opportunities are compliance-controlled.
- Cold WhatsApp, misleading claims and unauthorized pricing are prohibited.
