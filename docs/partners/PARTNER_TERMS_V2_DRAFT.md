# Dealix Partner Terms V2 — DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW

Status: INTERNAL DRAFT. Not published, not accepted terms, not legal advice.

## 1. Relationship

Partner is an independent business participant, not an employee, agent with signing authority,
or representative authorized to bind Dealix. No exclusivity unless separately signed.

## 2. Commercial authority

Partner may introduce and support qualified opportunities but may not:

- set or promise Dealix pricing, discounts, scope, delivery dates or acceptance criteria;
- issue a binding quote, invoice, contract, tender or guarantee on Dealix's behalf;
- promise revenue, ROI, compliance certification or regulatory approval;
- claim customer proof that has not been approved for publication.

Dealix commercial path remains: Free Execution Diagnostic -> Qualified Discovery ->
Customer-Specific Quote -> verified payment/start authority -> governed delivery -> proof.

## 3. Deal registration

Attribution starts only after Dealix accepts a non-duplicate registration. Standard protection
is 120 days; an evidence-backed active opportunity may be extended up to 180 days.
## 4. Commission basis

Commission is calculated on Net Commissionable Collected Revenue (NCCR), not contract value.
NCCR starts from cash actually collected by Dealix and excludes VAT, refunds, credits,
chargebacks, pass-through ad spend, licenses/vendor costs billed at cost, and government fees.

Services/projects use the approved motion rate. Eligible SaaS uses the approved recurring
rate for no more than the first 12 commissionable months unless a signed strategic agreement
states otherwise. Strategic services at up to 20% require margin approval before customer quote.

## 5. Clearing and payout truth

Default clearing period is 30 days after verified collection. A portal status of
`approved_for_payment` or `STAGED_NOT_PAID` is not proof that money was transferred.
Real payout requires an external payment execution receipt. Refunds/chargebacks may reduce
unpaid commission or create an offset under the final signed terms.

## 6. Multi-partner attribution

Where several approved partners materially contribute, Dealix may allocate an approved
commission pool by evidenced role. Default internal split is source 40%, qualification 20%,
close 40%. Duplicate registration does not create a second entitlement.
## 7. Marketing, privacy and channel rules

Partner must use truthful approved claims, disclose the commercial relationship where required,
and follow applicable privacy/marketing law. Consent evidence is required for direct marketing.
Cold WhatsApp under the Dealix brand is prohibited. Withdrawal/opt-out must be respected.
Public contact information alone is not treated as consent.

## 8. Government and tender opportunities

Government/tender deals are compliance-controlled. No commission may compensate a public
official/employee or any person for real or claimed influence over a public decision, award,
license, supply agreement or government process. Such opportunities remain on hold until review.

## 9. Brand, confidentiality and proof

Partner may use only approved Dealix assets and must protect confidential/customer information.
Customer logos, results, testimonials and case studies require publication authority and evidence.
Synthetic/demo proof must be labeled and never presented as customer proof.

## 10. Suspension and termination

Dealix may suspend attribution/enablement for spam, fraud, abuse, duplicate manipulation,
misleading claims, consent violations, government-influence risk, or other material breach.
Final termination, clawback, tax, dispute and governing-law clauses require Saudi legal review
before these terms become binding or public.
